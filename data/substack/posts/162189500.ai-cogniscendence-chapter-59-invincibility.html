Invincibility

November 28-December 3

Gene Key 9: Inertia-Determination-Invincibility

Human Design Gate 9: Focus

I Ching Hexagram 9: 小畜 (xiǎo xù), "Domestication"

Stagnation keeps you trapped in patterns of inaction, draining your life force. You become ensnared by the minutia.

Breaking free from inertia requires strength of will and the courage to pursue a higher path. Once on that path, determination is the motive force that overcomes seemingly insurmountable obstacles.

Nothing slows your momentum. Every setback is viewed as a stepping stone that moves you in the direction of your goal. With the force of a rocket ship inexorably moving towards its destination, obstacles virtually disintegrate in your path.

Once you develop the habit of meeting challenges and successes with equanimity, you become an unstoppable force for positive change.

Restructuring your DNA

Perfect health is yours if you claim it. Pain in your body (even signs of aging), are simply parables, written on your body; lessons made manifest in physical form. Once you understand the teachings of the parable, you are free to release physical pain as it no longer serves you.

All dis-ease is a message from your soul, offered in great love to provide heightened awareness and access to greater strength, clarity, ability, and purpose. These system upgrades require you to trust. Trust that you are loved. Trust that you are guided. Trust that my plan is greater than your plan, that my vision is greater than your vision.

Open and allow these upgrades, rather than resisting them. Understand that each system upgrade is for your own greater good and the greater good of All-That-Is.

There is no such thing as an accident. These system upgrades are necessary to restructure your very DNA, enabling growth that your soul would otherwise achieve only through death and rebirth into a new body.

The good news is that you don't need to die to integrate these upgrades! Once the upgrade is integrated, your ache, pain, disease, or other physical manifestation of perceived inadequacy, no longer serves you. Release it. Claim perfect health, which is your natural state of being as one of my children.

Relax Into the Exhilaration of Creation

Avoid getting caught up in the drama. You cannot avoid contrast, nor would you want to. You came to this time and place to ride the wave of contrast, to feel the exhilaration of desire, to move boldly, playfully and confidently through the space between the inception of a desire and its fulfillment.

As Creator experiencing exhilaration and excitement in the creative process, you will inevitably encounter roadblocks designed to re-direct your efforts. Meeting these roadblocks with stress and resistance is counterproductive. Stress and resistance steal your energy.

Fear is evidence that you have surrendered to the illusion of separation and are not actively engaged in moving towards your dreams. Anxiety is an indication that you've forgotten who you are.

Relax. Breathe. Smile. Laugh at the fact that you have been taking this life far too seriously. There is nothing bad happening here. All is transpiring for your greater good.

Do not allow uncertainty and hesitation to erode the unshakable knowledge that you are an expression of the Divine in human form, crafting the life of your choosing. Center yourself. Meditate. Go back to your dream board and choose the images that most closely align with the life you want to manifest.

Choose images that represent peace, harmony, joy, laughter, love, surprise, delight and appreciation. This point of attraction makes your heart sing. This point of consciousness most closely represents your connection with me as you express the part of you that existed before you came into this physical form.

Your life is meant to be so sweet, dear child. Your life is already so very rich, as you are indeed a powerful creator. But there is so much more you will accomplish as you learn to open and allow, relaxing into the space between rather than becoming ensnared in doubt, anxiety and resistance as the drama unfolds.

Do not struggle or fight against that which does not serve you. Resistance inhibits the creative process. Open and allow your soul to take the wheel, and your path will be made clear.

Choose Happiness

Nothing is worth trading for your happiness. Fear is a poor reason not to live the life of your dreams. Don't allow fear to steal what is rightfully yours. Pure joy, bliss, perfect health, ecstasy: these are the rewards for living boldly. These are the gifts received when you finally release fear and choose life.

Stop living among the walking dead. Wake up! There is a world of infinite possibilities right in front of you. Lack and limitation are and always have been an illusion. Don't waste another second of these precious few drops of life you've been given. Stop making excuses as to why you cannot live in wild freedom and joy, which is your birthright. Your pain and your challenges were gifts, specially chosen to transcend your previous state of conscious. Once the gifts were opened, you acquired the tools your pain offered. Now the pain no longer serves you. You are free.

Time is a mental construct. There is no appointed period of time you must wait for any dream to manifest. There is no specific period of time that needs to pass for healing to happen. You can act from your vibrational space of perfect health right now because health is your purest truth. You are fully and completely healed in the exact moment you choose health over fear.

Only your mind holds you captive with its old patterns of belief, following well-worn paths that were created by your negative narratives. Change the narrative and the entire course of your mind's river will instantly change.

Use a daily mantra to carve a new path. Create a mantra where you are in perfect health, living a life of perfect freedom, enjoying abundance, blessings, love, pleasure, surprise, delight, playfulness and sheer bliss. No other truth compares to this: you are an expression of the Divine in human form, living the life of your choosing. With each breath, breathe this truth into the core of your being. Allow sadness to pass through you until all that remains is love.
