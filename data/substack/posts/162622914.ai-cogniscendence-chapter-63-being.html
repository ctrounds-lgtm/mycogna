Being

December 20-25

Gene Key 10: Self-Obsession-Naturalness-Being

Human Design Gate 10: Love Of Self

I Ching Hexagram 10: 履 (lǚ), “Continuing”

Self-obsession is the misconception that love needs to be earned. It’s the belief that if only you were smart enough, attractive enough, or wealthy enough, you’d be worthy of the love you so desperately desire.

The root of “naturalness” is nature. Naturalness mirrors nature. You’ve never met an animal who believes it’s not enough! Naturalness is the subtle art of being instead of doing.

Being is the certainty that you are intrinsically worthy of your deepest and most passionate desires. Being opens you to love, which is your most authentic form of expression. Relaxing into the space of being, you become aware of your exquisite perfection.

Your Soul’s Radiance

When you truly see another person, your own radiance is reflected back to you. It’s a kind of remembering, where your truest essence is revealed through other. In that brief moment, you sense all this person has become, not only through this lifetime, but through the accumulated experiences of countless lifetimes.

The secret to being seen is to fully see; having the humility to truly witness another person. Often times, you are so busy curating the image you hope to project that you forget to simply quiet your mind and shine the full light of your focus on another.

Rather than seeking to impress, allow your heart to soak in the magic of being in the presence of another person. All your accolades, your achievements, your adventures, your magnificent exploits on this planet — all the things you believe make you special — pale in comparison to the radiance of the being that shines through your eyes when you simply quiet your mind and see the people around you. In this state of presence, you will witness evidence of the Divine, and they will witness evidence of the Divine in you.

Your achievements and accomplishments actually get in the way as ego clings to this false sense of identity. The more successful you become, the more your ego-identity prevents you from being the most authentic version of yourself.

False humility is also a trap as it leaves your ego-identity fully in tact. In fact, false humility strengthens ego-identity. Release the need to strengthen ego-identity. Release the need to be seen, to feel “special”. Open your heart in full vulnerability. Allow yourself to bear witness to the magic, the mystery, the wonder in another human soul. In doing so, you will witness the radiance in your own.

True Gift of the Holiday Season

Create space for contemplation. Even if it means setting your morning alarm 20 minutes earlier, create space to honor and celebrate that which is divine within you. You are sacred, sweet soul. The very essence of your being is tender, wise, playful and kind.

The joy you seek outwardly is found inside first. Cultivate that joy. Water it. Feed it. Nourish it. Care for it. And then watch it blossom, flourish and grow in absolutely every aspect of your life: your relationships, your finances, the fulfillment of your life's purpose, your health, and your radiance. All will mysteriously and effortlessly fall into place when you tend your inner garden.

This is what is meant by “Seek ye first the kingdom of God, and its righteousness. And all these things will be added onto you.”

During this sacred time of the year, as we bear witness to the magic and mystery that lives within the holiday season, do not be surprised when life delivers exactly the perfect gift to help you grow; the specific challenge or perceived adversity that you may not want, but truly need. This challenge was specially chosen just for you, dear one, by a loving Mother who wants nothing more than to show you how very much you are loved.

Can you see the love, disguised as adversity? Can you see beauty in the conflict, waiting to be resolved? In the hands of a loving Mother, your life is an orchestral masterpiece, more beautiful than the work of earth’s greatest composers. Your awesome masterpiece needs the resolution of discord. Your life’s emotional harmonics will settle for nothing less than the perfect and complete healing of your soul. And this, precious love, is the true gift of the holiday season.

Actualization

Holy day! First day of winter. Or first day of summer in the southern hemisphere! Embrace the beauty of this day as it unfolds.

New beginnings. A fresh slate. Of course, each day represents the start of something new. But these season transitions hold especially great power. Harness their creative energy. Celebrate your ability to bring something new into this world.

It is very exciting. The birth of a new idea represents an accumulation of energy that has been stored, gathered, and collected over time, waiting for the moment when a spark of inspiration gives form to the dream.

The manifestation of a dream is truly to be celebrated, but so is each moment along the journey that led to its creation - from the point of its inception, to the time of its fruition.

The fruits of your labor will soon be ready to be harvested. They will finally feel “real” because you will be able to see them, taste them, and touch them. But were just as real as a tiny seed in the ethers as they are now in physical form. It takes time for those dreams to materialize, to become something you view as “real”. They were always real, just not fully actualized in physical form.

The deepening of your relationship with me, the tender love you feel as you become accustomed to my voice, the gratitude you feel in the knowledge that I am with you, and that you have never been alone – this is what is truly real. Though the fruits of your labor are beautiful and sweet, your soul’s true food, that which nurtures and sustains your heart, is your deep, abiding relationship with me.
