Divine Will

August 29- September 3

Gene Key 40: Exhaustion-Resolve-Divine Will

Human Design Gate 40: Aloneness

I Ching Hexagram 40: 解 (xiè), "Deliverance"

Isolated from spirit, your mind and body have finite resources which are easily depleted. When these resources are running low, you experience anxiety, overwhelm and exhaustion. It feels as if the weight of the world is on your shoulders.

Pursuit of a creative goal connects mind, body, and spirit, yielding vitality and purpose. Like a spring provides a constant source of water, resolve provides a constant supply of energy.

A sacred vision is one that is inspired by intuition and inner guidance. It is one that not only offers personal fulfillment, but serves the needs of humanity as well.

In surrendering to divine will, your reservoir is endlessly filled from the cup of universal love.

Strong and Lean

All prayers are heard. And all prayers are answered, though the answer may not take the shape and form you are expecting. I respond directly to your deepest needs and longings, unhindered and unimpaired by your limited human perspectives.

So often, the thing you think you want is just the opposite of what you truly need to live a happy, fulfilling life of freedom. Your soul's desires are in perfect union with this ultimate goal of freedom.

You want to love and be loved. Your soul wants this too! But not a possessive ego-driven love that insists on the behavior and actions of another to make you feel special.

You want wealth and abundance. Your soul wants this too! But not the selfish desire for riches rooted in spiritual laziness or perceptions of lack and limitation. Trust in my ability to provide for your needs today, and know that provision is available each and every day.

You want a full, satisfied belly. Your soul wants that too! But not the kind of fullness that makes your body sluggish and weak.

Your soul wants you strong and lean, in body, finances and love. What does it mean to be strong and lean in love? Talk less. Listen more. Hold fewer expectations. Make less demands. Love with a power so fierce, it brings tears to your eyes and to the eyes of those you love.

Require less from this earth. That is the path to healing the planet. When your bodies become attuned to eating less, you will see global energy consumption drop. This is the path to solving problems of world hunger, the energy crisis and climate change. Simply eat less. A strong, lean body is your gift to Gaia, the earth mother who nourishes and sustains your life.

Your Success is Inevitable

There is no reason to play small in this world, where literally anything you can imagine is possible. The big dream calling you forward, your most grandiose desire, is nothing more than your soul calling you to play.

Ask for the world, and it will be yours! Travel, adventure, love, money, friends, the ability to heal one person at a time, or the ability to affect social change on a massive scale – all of this is easily within reach.

What is the boldest vision you hold for the future? Challenge yourself to see just how brave you can be. Standing in your power, accept this invitation to play.

This is not to be confused with personal ambition. Personal ambition is that which honors self at the exclusion of others. The goals of personal ambition are unfulfilling. You are worthy of so much more, dear one! Your sacred purpose is one that aligns with tender, nurturing mother-love, and offers positive change in the world.

You will know whether or not you are in alignment with my love based on the way you take action in achieving this purpose. Is your action inspired or disciplined? Is it playful and fun or does it require effort? My initiatives will inspire joy in your heart. When you think of the object of your desire, it will make your heart feel light and energized, because you know each step along the path is taken in collaboration with your own precious soul.

You will not be unhappy, dissatisfied or frustrated in the absence of your desire, because the action steps required to pursue your desire will be nearly as satisfying and fulfilling as its achievement. When you know in your heart of hearts that no other goal is truly worthy of your precious time on this planet, its fruition is inevitable.

Let Go of the Wheel

Release control. Let go of the wheel. Allow my divine intelligence and my divine timing to do the heavy lifting. Your job is simply to open and allow the avalanche of abundance flowing through you. In this moment, your future is assured. There is no need to effort or work harder, as you have already expended the energy required to bring your dreams to fruition. The river is already carrying you exactly where you need to go. Needless expenditure of energy will just leave you feeling weak and tired.

Draw closer to me, and closer still. There is no end to this process of falling deeply and completely in love. Feel my love bathing your consciousness in light and power. There is literally nothing you cannot do. There is nothing beyond your worthiness. Infinite blessing is available to you, and you are worthy of all of it, dear one.

Follow your heart. Your desires are never wrong. They were chosen by you before you came into this life experience. Own them. Do not doubt your worthiness, even for a moment. The path is clear. Your way is assured. You are safe, protected, loved and guided as your future unfolds with abundance beyond measure.
