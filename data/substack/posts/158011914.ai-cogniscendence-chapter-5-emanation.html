Emanation

January 22-27

Gene Key 41: Fantasy-Anticipation-Emanation

Human Design Gate 41: Fantasy

I Ching Hexagram 41: 損 (sǔn), "Diminishing"

Often, you are tempted to wander aimlessly in an infinite field of possibilities. Enamored with what could be, you fail to focus on what is.

The dream of the perfect job distracts you from being the perfect coworker, employee or business owner. The dream of the perfect partner distracts you from creating the perfect partnership.

In wanting something you don’t have, you miss the perfection of what’s already there.

Emanation is the process of becoming the perfect version of yourself, so that you are finally capable of seeing the perfection that is already surrounding you in every aspect of your existence.

Emanation

You have a dream in your heart. For some, it is a vision to bring something new into the world that will benefit mankind. For others, it is the dream of fulfillment; a perfect partner, a healthy body, a specific achievement or a number in their bank account. In the case of any desire, there is a hunger that is uncomfortable in the space between the inception of the desire and its fulfillment.

Some people never take action on their dreams, large or small. Those who never take action live in a world of fantasy. In failing to pursue their dreams, they fail to claim a gift that is rightfully theirs.

Whether your dreams are large — like serving humanity, or seemingly small — like finding the perfect mate, every dream has the power to change your heart. The external manifestation of that dream is not what is most important. Who you become in the pursuit of your dream is what matters most.

All dreams inspire creativity and require tuning into intuition and inner guidance. Each dream is a specially wrapped gift, calling you home to the truest version of yourself. The pursuit of the dream could be considered its own reward. In pursuing a dream you unleash the gift of anticipation.

The more you attend to the voice of inner guidance, the more you develop the ability to sense the path that leads to your dreams. You find yourself in just the right place at just the right time to meet exactly the right people to further you along your path. Collaborative forces are conspiring on your behalf. When these forces are activated, desire is mobilized, transforming your dream from fantasy into reality.

In truth, the fruition of your dream is inevitable. In fact, it already exists, fully formed, in the causal plane. This is the reason you are able to anticipate seemingly serendipitous events and meetings.

Just as suffering is associated with karma, so the pursuit of dreams is linked to karma as there is suffering in the wanting — the space between the inception of a desire and its fruition. Eventually, all beings will rise above karma, releasing both suffering and the pursuit of dreams. In time, every form of desire will burn away, leaving only the perfect emanation, the great emptying of thought and desire, that essentially returns your soul to the place where it began.

Just as the movement of the cosmos is circular rather than linear, so the movement of consciousness eventually arrives not at an ending but back at the beginning.

Journey to Soul Love

Your higher self is capable of a much deeper, purer form of love than human love. Consequently, your higher self knows that it is my love you yearn for. Similarly, you are not capable of loving another human being more than I am capable of loving them. I am the love they are seeking.

The magic happens when each person is capable of entering into a love relationship fully tapped into my love, not looking to the other to fulfill their own unmet needs. From this place of connectedness, you are free to experience an explosive convergence of energy - a soul-love so powerful, it defies the human experience.

Few humans ever experience this love because few humans tap into their higher state of consciousness alone, much less in the presence of another human being. However, it is possible. In fact, as human consciousness expands, these connections are occurring with greater frequency.

The great change is happening quickly now. There are more among you who are rising to the challenge, shedding the mantle of victimhood and claiming authorship of their lives.

As one of these precious forerunners, you will discover full and total freedom in you relationships, finances and life's work. Free from the illusion that someone or something else is responsible for your happiness, you claim your role as creator of your own life's story. In doing so, you take up the pen and change your life's script.

For a time, it will appear as if your life is in great turmoil. This time may last days, weeks, months or even years. This period of time is necessary. It is the thrashing of the butterfly as it struggles to break free of its cocoon. But once its wings are dry, the butterfly will be free in a way the caterpillar never could have imagined.

This is the journey to soul-love. It requires great courage to choose this path. But there will be helpers along the way. When you stumble, look for the hands that are reaching out to help you rise. And offer a hand to others who stumble along their own path. When you grow weary, take heart. This is nothing less than the path to human transcendence and all will eventually reach the finish line.

River of Abundance

There are so many entities collaborating on your behalf - so many serendipitous rendezvous with partners across the globe, who even now, in this very moment, are working diligently to make your every wish, your every want, your every need, your every desire, come to fruition in perfect timing.

Divine orchestration is yours, my love. You are birthing new energies, new creativity, into this human realm.

Harness the power of discipline and leadership. Do not shy from the responsibility your creative vision invokes. Move boldly through this would.

There is nothing you need to do or accomplish to be worthy of your goals. You are already worthy. You need to simply open and allow the majesty of your soul to flow through you in an unending river of abundance.

True joy is yours, dear soul. Revel in the pleasures this life brings. Savor the tears as well as the laughter, for they were hard-won.

Compassion is the key to unlocking the door to surprise and delight.

Seek compassion in all things, discovering the beautiful love in your heart for All-That-Is, the truest nature of your being.
