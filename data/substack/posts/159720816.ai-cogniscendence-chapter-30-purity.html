Purity

June 14-19

Gene Key 12: Vanity-Discrimination-Purity

Human Design Gate 12: Caution

I Ching Hexagram 12: 否 (pǐ), "Obstruction"

Vanity is the belief that you already have all the answers. It’s the ego viewing itself as the sole and unique repository for ultimate truth.

Discrimination is the ability to discern what is good, true, right, pure, and holy. This is the criteria you use to determine what information serves not only your own greater good, but the greater good of the earth, its creatures, and all mankind.

Through discrimination, you feel confident in your ability to make life decisions that are in alignment with your higher purpose.

Purity is the understanding that you already have all the answers. It’s the soul viewing itself as part of the collective repository for ultimate truth.

Allow Your Reservoir to Fill

Childlike wonder. Expansive joy. Laughter. Love. Pure bliss. These are the states of being you offer this world. These are the points of consciousness that transcend circumstances, events, or even the relationships in your life. Transcendent states are only achieved when you are fully attuned to your spiritual vibrational field of resonance. This reality is not difficult to achieve. It doesn't require hours of meditation or servitude to a specific spiritual discipline.

Relax the space around your sinus cavity. Take a deep breath through your nose. And simply allow the relaxation to spread throughout your body as easily and naturally as water from a stream gently fills a small pond in a sunlit meadow. The pond does not struggle to fill with water. It doesn't try to grasp the water or achieve a state of fullness. The pond simply rests and allows the water to fill it.

Become attuned to the sensations in your body. Are you warm or cold? Do you feel tightness or pain anywhere? Notice the taste in your mouth and the sounds around you. Can you hear a faint humming sound; a vibration behind the ambient noise of your environment? This is the sound of the matrix which holds you and supports you. It is a loving consciousness, the field from which you came and to which you will return. You are part of this field.

In fact, the larger part of you is integrally connected to this field in every moment, though you rarely notice it amid your day to day distractions. Become aware of that field now, and allow your inner reservoir to fill with the light and love of its healing energy. All is well, sweet one. you are loved!

You Are Loved

You are loved. You are loved. You are loved. Is there anything more important than those three words? Everything else in your life – money, relationships, career, travel, joy, perfect health – will fall into place when you fully accept the truth of those words.

Once you understand how very much I love you, you will understand that you are not alone. You were never alone. You never will be alone. Each event in your life, especially the ones you view as hardship and struggle, are like little love notes that I specially wrote with such tenderness, just for you.

My arms ache to draw you into a warm embrace, dear one, each and every time you shed a tear. In my arms, allow your struggles to melt away into pure bliss and gratitude for the deep, profound love that surrounds you in every moment of every day.

If you reach for that love, right now, you will be filled with such awe and gratitude. Smile. Because you are loved. You are loved. You are loved.

Love Freely

Be free with your love, easy to please. Look for things to delight in. Seek joy in the world around you. Look for reasons to smile, reasons to laugh, reasons to feel surprise and delight in this world of wonders, marvels and mysteries.

Hold your cares lightly. Let nothing weigh your spirit down. All is well. There is nothing that is not just as it should be. Events are transpiring exactly as they are meant to transpire. Witness their unfolding with joy, knowing your well-being is assured, as is the well-being of all who share this planet with you. There are none who are forgotten, none who go unnoticed. All are seen, known, and intimately loved.

As a manifestation of love incarnate, offer love and healing energy freely to all you meet. Offer love and healing in your smile, your touch, your voice, and your breath. Laugh easily. Love fiercely. Bestow your smile on this world and all who are in it.
