Beauty

November 6-11

Gene Key 1: Entropy-Freshness-Beauty

Human Design Gate 1: Self Expression

I Ching Hexagram 1: 乾 (qián), "Force"

Entropy is an addiction to novel experiences. It is the chaotic energy that drives you to look for the next adventure, the next rush, the next high, without allowing the magic and beauty of the moment to pierce your heart.

Freshness is the ability to experience the world anew each day. A cup of coffee, a house plant, warm water running down your body, soft sunlight playing on the carpet, the most mundane things in your daily surroundings are a source of limitless pleasure and delight.

The entire world mirrors the Mother’s love. With playful curiosity, gaze into the eyes of the people you meet. In awe, you will see the profound beauty of the Divine, reflecting your own radiant beauty back to you.

Healing Ego-Wounds

Beauty is intrinsic to your nature. Beauty is woven into the DNA, the very fabric of creation. The extent to which you see the beauty, feel the beauty, breathe the beauty, absorb the beauty, is the extent to which you are in alignment with the true nature of reality and the truest manifestation of Spirit in this physical form.

Nothing on this planet is excluded from this beauty because all things are indigenous to the earth and the amazing perfection of the molecular structures that support it. Each atom is a work of incomparable beauty and majesty. And the space between each electron is alive with an intelligence so profound, it mystifies the wisest sage.

This is the true "you". You are not the thoughts you think about yourself and others. You are not the cumulative experiences or perspectives acquired during this brief and often shallow life experience. You are neither your accomplishments nor your shortcomings. You are the being that witnesses all of it, watching the drama as it unfolds, assisting in the cathartic release of blocks that impede your healing.

These blocks are linked to egoic structures. Become aware of the ego-identity associated with those blocks and you'll release the energy resisting your natural flow. Your natural state of being is one of joy, peace, laughter, love, surprise, and delight. These are the gifts you offer yourself and the planet when you allow your ego-wounds to softly and gently heal.

If you fail to find beauty in anything on earth, examine your heart and locate the egoic structure preventing you from seeing the beauty in every single person, place, creature, and event on this magnificent planet.

Not A Moment is Wasted

It really is beautiful, isn't it? This life you are living? Feel the deep, harmonious resonance in your chest, reverberating back to your first heart beat and forward to the moment when you take your last breath; a single note, sounding throughout the entirety of this lifetime. All your perceived hardships are inconsequential. They simply provide the tools to create your masterpiece, a spectacularly beautiful patch on a great quilt that its stitched together by the hands of time.

Your beautiful contribution to this universal tapestry is significant. It is sacred, holy and true. In fact, there is no such thing as a wasted moment in a life. Each moment, even the ones we view as inconsequential or even detrimental, are of unfathomable worth. Not a single heartbeat is wasted. Not a single breath is drawn in vain. Your being-ness is what is sacred. You may not be aware of the note your soul is singing, the light you are emitting, the frequency that is joining with the other frequencies of all things living and non-living.

But that frequency is critical to the balance of universal harmonics. Like the destabilizing influence of an atom without a balanced number of electrons, your absence would cause destabilization in the field of universal consciousness.

Matter cannot be created nor destroyed. In that great mysterious triad of life, sex and death, the essence of your sound goes on, traveling from one lifetime into the next.

True Power Lies In Surrender

True power lies in surrender. Surrender to the love that is around you now. Do you realize how much effort you've been expending to keep the truth of that love away? All your struggle, all your pain, all your aching, your yearning for things which have not yet manifest, are keeping the truth of my love, the One Love, from permeating your heart, mind, and spirit.

Surrender to my love. Rest, sweet child, in the tenderness of my embrace. In the safety of my arms, you will finally see yourself as whole, complete, and fully satisfied. Right now, in this moment, you have access to the fullness of your perfection, the recognition of your divine beauty in human form. And you are so beautiful to me, precious child. Your sweet spirit is so willing to embark on new adventures in this beautiful life you've been given.

Life is truly a precious gift. But do not be concerned that you are squandering this gift. Those thoughts are rooted in guilt and shame, which have no place in the unfolding of this delicious life experience of your choosing. Living is the purpose of life. It really is that simple. The more you open your awareness to this truth, the more you will see your life as I see it - prefect, beautiful, a divine dance that is being performed with exquisite strength and balance.

Each movement you perceive as a mis-step is executed with majesty, beauty and grace. This dance has been perfectly choreographed by your soul to contribute to your greatest good and the greatest good of all beings on earth.

You are so radiant, dear one. Know that you are both love and loved.
