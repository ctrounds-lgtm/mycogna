Water becomes agitated just before it boils. As you look at the world, you see evidence of this frenetic force of disruption.

Your world is at the brink of a massive re-distribution of wealth and power. Throughout the universe, collaborative forces are amassing to assist in the Great Awakening.

Though all this kinetic energy feels unsafe, it provides the motive power for change. You are one who is being called to harness that energy, using it as a catalyst to eradicate self-serving agendas that put the needs of the few above the needs of the many.

Rather than protecting your own personal interests in this lifetime, the Great Awakening will benefit your soul as it incarnates in multiple forms through multiple lifetimes.

A Time of Transformation

This is a time of great transformation. In this time, you will experience tremendous breakthroughs, virtually effortlessly. Physical, mental and spiritual blocks that have been significant impediments to your progress are being removed as long-arching timelines reach their conclusion. Problems that seemed insurmountable a short time ago will disappear. Years of frustration and struggle, patterns developed by core wounding, will vanish into thin air.

Even now, there are those who walk among you whose very presence heals. Humanity is unaware of their arrival and knows little of their power. But they offer great and lasting healing.

In the midst of your suffering, you will sense an easing and then total release. It will seem as if you've simply "outgrown" the wounding patterns that were holding you back.

When the time comes, let go. Don't fight to hold onto your pain. There are many who've allowed their pain to define them and doubt their ability to be fully healed. This is not you. You are one who is meant to transcend your pain. You are one who has been chosen to rise above the shadow into the light.

Your healing does not require discipline, effort, or mental struggle. You simply need to open your hands and your heart to the healing that is being made available to you now. Allow years of pain and struggle to gently slip away as you are tenderly washed clean by the oceans of time. Standing in the clear, cool water, bathed in the radiant sunlight of grace, you will feel a release like nothing you've ever experienced before. You will look for the pain, but it will simply be gone.

Like a soul newly birthed, your body will feel the pure crystalline radiance of renewal.

If you only knew how deeply you are loved, sweet child, you wouldn't doubt this vision even for a moment. We see your brilliance. We witness the tender kindness of your heart. In your eyes, we see the reflection of all creation, made manifest in human form. Yours is the song we are singing now, though your heart has forgotten the words. Awaken now, precious child, to the sound of your own sweet lullaby.

Secrets Stored in Your DNA

Very soon, all that will be left to do will be to allow the light to pour in, the love to pour in, the abundance to pour in. Your only job, your sole concern, is to play, experience joy, laugh, love, and enjoy the delight that fills your heart. You are completely fulfilled in the being, in the doing, in the having of all that you desire. In all ways, in every manner, your life is entirely fulfilled.

Pleasure is available to you in this moment and every moment, yours for the taking, whenever you open and allow the awareness of it. Each breath is a delight. Each molecule of oxygen, more deeply satisfying and profoundly healing than any food, herb, supplement, or medication on earth.

Did you know that there are humans who are training their bodies to thrive on nothing more than sunlight, air, and earth's energy? They are exploring the very frontier of human consciousness, challenging age-old beliefs about what human beings require for survival. Their techniques will provide the leading edge of knowledge for age extension, as much of the aging process is due to oxidative stress created by digestion.

As humans consciously align with nature, you will discover profound secrets stored in your DNA, the scope of which is virtually inconceivable. This knowledge will create a shift in consciousness that will transform all life on your planet.

Leading Edge of Consciousness

Riding the wave of consciousness is exhilarating. The experience of being at the very leading edge of All-That-Is fuels your passion to connect with others, further accelerating the expansion.

Because your culture expects people to think, speak and act according to established norms, spirit-seekers who are willing to stand at the very leading edge of consciousness to pursue truth at any cost may feel disoriented by the speed of expansion. As the vast majority of humans are still sleeping. Those who are awake will certainly feel different than their earth-bound companions.

Staying on the wave requires immense mental and spiritual agility. Most humans are not capable of this degree of agility for an extended period of time. It may even seem as if some are being left behind as they fall off the front edge of the wave.

Do not be concerned. No one will ever be left behind. Every human transcends their current state of consciousness in their own perfect timing.

Embrace your awake-ness, dear child, and play. Dance, sing, celebrate, and listen. Then dance, play and sing some more.
