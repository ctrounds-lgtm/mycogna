Invisibility

December 9-14

Gene Key 26: Pride-Artfulness-Invisibility

Human Design Gate 26: The Trickster

I Ching Hexagram 26: 大畜 (dà xù), “Potential Energy”

Pride commands attention through force of will as opposed to strength of character. It manipulates others for personal gain. Pride demands to be seen.

Artfulness is born of grace and love, which draws the eye by virtue of its beauty. Leveraging charisma and strength of presence, you inspire others to invest time, creativity, and ingenuity to build a brighter future.

You hold the vision of a world where all mankind lives in harmony and abundance. Though you have no interest in being seen, others can’t keep their eyes off of you.

Invisibility is the ethereal magnificence of a star whose brilliance cannot be seen, but only imagined.

Invisibility

There is great freedom in invisibility. While so many clamber to be seen, yearning for recognition, you are content to stay in the shadows.

You have great ingenuity that can be harnessed for the greater good. Conspiring behind the scenes, you do the quiet inner work that benefits all mankind.

Your satisfaction comes from working alongside collaborative universal forces that are creating change on this planet. As a spirit-worker, you are one who heals not only your own core wounds, but the deep core wounds plaguing the earth.

In silence, you retreat to an inner universe, which is just as expansive as the outer cosmos. Like steering a spaceship, you traverse great distances in this inner realm, journeying deep into the roots of pain, the ancient wellspring from which all human suffering emerges. Offering love and light in this inner sanctum, you transmute pain to love and heal all of mankind.

This is the work of the planetary guardians. The labor is invisible and can never be known or understood by humanity. This is the soul-mending that heals not only humans, but plants, animals, and all the earth. This is the work of the Pachamama, the Earth Mother.

It is intriguing to note that this is the gift you receive when you transcend pride and selfishness. Rather than hoarding resources for your own personal benefit, rather than seeking validation for achievements, or accolades for accomplishments that may not even be yours, rather than manipulating others to conform to your agenda, you harness your great ingenuity to serve the planet.

As your vibration sings the earth into harmony, all the secrets of the cosmos will reveal themselves to you and the unknown will be made known.

An Invitation to Play

The biggest dreams are the ones that activate the broadest range of universal forces. Your collaborators in the unseen love to rise to the challenge!

The size of your dream is a reflection of the depth of your faith. Big dreams are so rewarding because in both the inception of the desire and the pursuit of that desire, you become skilled in the practice of soul embodiment, yielding a deeper, richer relationship with your own precious soul.

Do not shy away from the magnificent dream your soul has placed in your heart. It is nothing more — and nothing less -—than an invitation to play. Accept that invitation fearlessly, knowing that divine assistance will be guiding you and inspiring you every step of the way.

Power

Intriguing, isn't it? Do you see how your desires shift and change as you evolve? Your journey is a grand adventure, a constant collaboration with forces you cannot see, endlessly conspiring on your behalf to bring about that which is of your choosing and contributes to the world's greater good.

The glory of the mystery is truly awesome. The deep well of magic is endless. The more you open and allow, the more you draw the object of your desire to you.

There is no such thing as a dream that is too big. In fact, I revel in the opportunity to rise to a challenge. Step into the fullness of your power and feel the full magnitude of energy available to you.

For me, $100,000 is as easy as $10. Perfect health is as easy as mending a cut. Finding the love of a lifetime is as easy as finding love for a moment. Your work is simply to put yourself in the vibrational space to allow miracles to flow.

Smile. Laugh. Romp. Play. Spin. Dance. Sing. Hum. Move your hands. Revel in the joy that is rightfully yours. Play. Play. Play. Then play some more!
