I Ching Hexagram 53: 漸 (jiàn), "Advancement"

Just as a single mature oak tree produces thousands of acorns that will create a new generation of trees, a single dream can change the lives of millions.

The Great Awakening

This is the time to gather in communities of like-minded people who are sharing in this tremendous experience of physical, mental, and spiritual expansion. You are truly becoming super-human as your body, mind, and spirit are capable of things that have never been possible before.

New spectrums of light, new vibrational frequencies, are arriving on your planet every day. This creates great turbulence. But for those who are able to tap into these new energy sources, there is virtually limitless potential for expansion, healing, and growth.

Expansion of this magnitude is disorienting. You will benefit from sharing with others who are experiencing circumstances and events that do not seem physically possible. And in truth, just a short time ago, only a small handful of people on the planet were able to achieve these higher states of being. All that is changing now.

Listen to those who share stories of strange animal encounters, invincibility, spontaneous healing, interaction with non-human sentient beings, and visions or spiritual journeys. You may experience odd sensations in your body, like vertigo, hearing a high-pitched hum / buzz, or a surge in sexual energy as your body adapts to new higher frequencies.

These are signs of the Great Awakening, which is dawning now on your planet. The wave will continue to build in strength and magnitude as many others are added to your number. The awakening can be confusing and even frightening as the changes make you doubt your sanity. But as you boldly share your experiences, you will provide a safe haven for others who are going through their own awakening and are in need of comfort, reassurance and community.

In time, as more join your ranks, these changes will become commonplace. This will be the tipping point where fear gives way to love. Then none will stand against the tide of love that sweeps the planet, engulfing pain, loneliness, fear, and hatred in a sea of beauty and peace. In the midst of these turbulent times, take heart in the knowledge that there will indeed be a morning that dawns on a planet free from suffering. That is the vision you manifest through your own awakening, dear one.

What Mountains Will You Move Today?

Isn't the world amazing? Do you see how incredible this life is? Nature is all around you. Even in the densest concrete jungle, little plants manage to spring up through cracks in the pavement. Within the smallest plant, miracles unfold!

A single sapling contains millions of cells that can turn the light from the sun into energy. Imagine that! Turning the sun's light into energy! The tiny sapling then uses that energy to grow roots that will strain against the concrete, breaking it down, widening the crack that allows it to gain access to more soil, more water, more sun, more space.

If a sapling can do this using only sunlight, what great feats can you accomplish with your beautiful mind, your powerful heart, your strong will, and your magnificent dreams? What mountains will you move today? What planetary shifts will you create?

The world needs your greatness. Now, more than ever, your passion, playfulness, and wild sense of freedom are required. You are being called to step onto life's stage, to play your role as healer, encourager, lover, wanderer, creator.

Many in this world are feeling fear and great uncertainty. Do you hear the call to leadership? Maybe its in your family or your community. Maybe it's in your country or in the world. While the size of the stage is irrelevant, the role you play is of the utmost importance.

Like the sapling whose roots have the strength to break pavement, the roots of your love have the strength to crack the hard outer shell preventing light from entering a human heart. As a great opener of hearts, you are being called now to take center stage. And we will be watching with elation as you give the performance of a lifetime!

Your Gifts Are Desired

Collaborate. Seek community with like-minded spiritual adventurers. Do not shy from taking a leadership roll when there is a void. This void has been created just for you as a means of teaching you to step into your power.

Your gifts are desired. Your unique talents and skills are needed and wanted. There is a calling that only you can hear; a dream placed in your heart that only you can fulfill.

You will recognize this dream as the thing that makes your spirit come alive when you think about it. This dream energizes you as it calls you forward. As you deepen into your relationship with me, the size and scope of the dream will not feel overwhelming and the necessary action steps will reveal themselves in logical, orderly fashion.

Listen to your soul when it instructs you to just lie in bed or sit in meditation. Open your heart, receiving inspiration and renewal. Create space. This is the time when universal forces will be mobilized on your behalf, orchestrating collaborative energies.

Your actions are just the tiniest tip of the iceberg. But those specific soul-inspired actions are absolutely critical in creating momentum and leveraging universal powers.

If you only knew how very potent the seeds of your imagination are, you would be amazed that so little personal energy is required to make them grow.

Before long, those seeds will take on a life of their own, blessing the lives of millions.
