You know what you should be doing, but you don’t do it. Half-heartedness leads to failed businesses, failed marriages, environmental crisis and social instability. Over time, wasting time and energy leads to anxiety, burnout and depression.

Becoming crystal clear on our “why” helps to maintain a steady flow of energy. You have a virtually endless supply of vitality when you pursue a goal that is worthy of your time on this planet. Passion fuels commitment, inspiring others to take action.

Discipline is no longer necessary when action is driven by a joyful outpouring of love. Devotion is an act of unconditional loves, where a simple act of service is its own reward.

Devotion

The greatest force holding back the evolution of human consciousness is not conflict or divisiveness. It is not lack of awareness or understanding. It is not even fear, greed, pride or judgement. The single greatest force holding back your own personal evolution as well as the evolution of the planet is half-heartedness.

Half-heartedness sees a problem, but responds with apathy. Half-heartedness allows large corporations to make decisions that strip the earth of its natural resources. It paves the way for global warming. On a personal level, half-heartedness contributes to obesity and disease. It prevents conversations that could repair a broken marriage or reach a troubled child. Half-heartedness weakens the mind and destroys the spirit, opening the door to addictive behaviors.

In time, families and social structures implode as they are destroyed from the inside out. Like an apple tree with a rotten trunk, its leaves look healthy but it produces little or no fruit.

The remedy for half-heartedness is commitment. Focus on one small area of your life. Like apathy, commitment spreads. As you commit to even the smallest cause, you will be amazed at the way commitment infuses other areas of your life.

Truly devote yourself to your relationships. Many believe that their partner, not their own half-heartedness, is the reason their relationship doesn't feel fulfilling. Many believe the love for their partner has died. Marriages end because one or both partners think they've fallen out of love.

Small, simple acts of devotion re-kindle the spark, not only in their partner's heart, but in their own heart as well. In long term relationships, love needs to be cultivated. It requires daily commitment to kindness in thought, word, and deed.

If you struggle to even think something kind about your partner, much less doing something for them, try turning it into a game. Each day, choose three simple ways you can express kindness. The next day, choose three different things. Do this for a week and watch the magic happen.

Devotion evokes a sweetness, a tenderness within your heart. Through devotion, you recognize that it is loving, not being loved, that offers true fulfillment.

Divine Grace

Devotion means different things to different people. To some it is an act of worship. To others it is the commitment to follow the voice of inner guidance, no matter the cost. To others, devotion is the dedication to show up over and over in the pursuit of an ideal. The human heart is wired for devotion.

Your attention to the word "devotion", your contemplation on how to integrate it into your life, is what is being asked of you now. Contemplating devotion will yield a greater awareness of the nature of my love, which feeds your soul and sustains your being.

The more time you spend in my presence, the more easily and effortlessly you will forget the things that divide and remember the common goodness, rightness, and love that unites all who pursue nature's universal truths.

In contemplating devotion, you see the wonder and beauty that lies at the very heart of every religion and spiritual practice. From this perspective, these religious and spiritual practices are awe-inspiring and majestic in their simplicity and grace. Every one of them offers a unique path that leads to precisely the same destination.

Devotion is the act of bringing heaven to earth. It is the means by which each and every human rises above his or her struggles and clasps my hand as it reaches out to him/her. Your life's challenges were lovingly created for this very reason: to remind you of that loving hand. The deeper, richer and wilder your challenges are, the more profoundly my grace will saturate your cells and permeate your bones.

Explore what devotion means to you. Attend to the quiet tug in your heart to commune with me. Create space in your day to honor the light that lives within you; the light that inspires the song – the unique tone – only your precious heart can sing.

Structure and Routine

People crave routine. As much as you enjoy being in new, exciting locations, you crave structure in your days as structure allows you to feel safe.

This structure changes to accommodate the seasons of life. And of course you should resist structures that prevent you from following your heart's calling.

But structure provides a sense of satisfaction and lists provide a sense of completion.

Make a short journal entry at the end of each day to celebrate your accomplishments. Recognize your own achievements and congratulate yourself for a job well done.

Make human connections a part of your daily "to-do''s". Gather with friends. Reach out to family members. Make an effort to connect and celebrate their accomplishments as well. This will provide context and meaning to both your days and theirs.
