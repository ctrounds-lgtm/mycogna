Stress is created when priorities are misaligned. In some cases it manifests as busyness. In other cases it manifests as chaotic thoughts. Stress results in wasted energy as you place undue emphasis on the minutia of everyday life.

Restraint is the discipline to maintain right thought and action by refusing to get caught up in life’s many dramas. Creating space for rest and contemplation, you consciously choose which actives are the best use of your precious time and energy.

Stillness is the dormant energy of the seed as it lies buried in the earth. It is alive with possibility. Stillness is the resting energy required for growth. It is the space required for insight, creativity and breakthroughs. Every new idea exists in the stillness of formlessness before taking shape in physical form.

Be Still

You will naturally gravitate towards your old beliefs and thought patterns if you don't provide space to just be in stillness.

Be o.k. without having the answers or even a clear path forward. Find solace in the stillness, the emptiness, and take baby steps forward only when you feel balanced and ready.

Relax into the not-knowing. All things are transpiring as they are meant to transpire, in their perfect timing. Sometimes silence is an indication that I am still in the process of aligning collaborative components that will contribute to your greater good and the greater good of All-That-Is. Be patient and relax into the stillness.

The Value of Stillness

Feel into the sacredness of the space between thought and movement. In this stillness, your inspiration for new adventures are born.

The stillness, as well as the movement, is sacred. Both are necessary for the beautiful unfolding of this magnificent life experience.

You are drawn to movement. But stillness has played an equally formative role in your expansion. It is your place of healing - physically, mentally and spiritually.

The practice of conscious stillness as well as conscious movement is valuable. Do not put undue emphasis on movement, or undervalue the importance of stillness. Both are equally important. Without the stillness to recharge, providing ample time in silence to listen to your soul, you would simply deplete your energy and burn out.

The quiet space inside your heart is every bit as worthy of honor as the adventure that shares that same heart-space. Thought and action. This is the ebb and flow that creates balance in this beautiful life's journey.

Peace In The Stillness

Feel the sacred breath of life flowing through you now, expanding into the space of all that is known and unknown. This holy breath brings with it ancient wisdom, knowledge that has been passed down through the many lifetimes you have lived. In the space between breaths, there is light, and that light is the place where my love dwells.

Inhabit that space and you will find rest for your weary heart. The solace of generations who have become before you reside there, waiting to offer peace, guidance, tranquility, and shelter from the storm.

This is what it means to be still and know that I am God.

In the stillness between breaths, I will find you, sweet one. I will softly call your name and wait for you to hear my voice, guiding you to that place of inner knowing, that place where all is well, just as it always has been and just as it always will be. Though the wind may buffet the water's surface, just beneath the waves, the deep water is still and unchanging. Peace waits for you in the stillness.

Lay perfectly still. Surround yourself with light. Allow that light to penetrate your body, reaching right through to the center of your center, the core of your being, where all my love and the hope it provides is right there waiting for you.
