The time for struggle is over. Challenge is necessary for growth. Struggle is not.

The Path of Honor

Challenge is necessary. Struggle is not. Many people believe that challenge and struggle are synonymous. Your challenges need not be painful.

Once you see that all of life is conspiring on your behalf, and that even your challenges are just blessings in disguise, you will approach each day with freshness, vitality, and hope.

Do you realize how boring life would be without challenges? A life without challenge would lead to gluttony and laziness. Your challenges are special gifts, chosen just for you, to hone you, shape you, and mold you into the person you need to be to accomplish what you came to this earth to accomplish.

Accomplishment in this sense is not to be confused with doing. Accomplishment has more to do with character than achievement.

When you embrace challenges rather than simply enduring them, you will begin to actually enjoy the game. In each and every case, the strategy to winning the game is the same: choose love. Ask yourself, "What does love look like in this moment?" Then transmute every challenge from discomfort to love. In time, layer upon layer of challenges will be purified and refined, encasing your heart in love.

Through your challenges, your heart is forged in the fire of grace, where all fear, pain, anger, and guilt are transmuted to love. This is the path of honor, the path of the true leader.

In following the path of honor, your own individual needs become subservient to love. In service to others, honor burns brightly in your heart, and the weight of personal challenges dissolves like mist in the morning sun.

You Are Safe

Gratitude. What are you thankful for in this moment? As you sit, relax and allow your mind to clear. Allow your heart to open. Consult inner guidance, asking the question, "What does my soul want me to know?" Then just sit and quietly listen. From that place of inner knowing, you will become aware of unseen blessings, gifts that may be going unrecognized.

Even in your bleakest hour, gifts abound. Do not discount them. Even if the recognition of your own strength and resilience is the only gift rising to your awareness, that alone is worth any pain or discomfort you may be experiencing. In bearing witness to your own strength, you recognize your power to heal. The same energy that allows you to heal is the energy that was used to create the cosmos. It is the creative force that brings order out of chaos, restoring balance in the universe.

Close your eyes. Feel the expansiveness of life-force energy, knowing it is the same energy that flows through your veins; the god-stuff from which you were created. Whenever you feel isolated, lonely or at the brink of despair, know that you are loved. You are worthy of love. You are known, seen, held, and cherished, precious one.

View your life through the eyes of a child, with innocence, wonder, and trust. No matter how things might appear, you are safe. Relax into that warm feeling of safety in this moment.

Fear that is not actionable is irrational. Take action to alleviate the conditions that frighten you. Then release the fear, as it no longer serves you. Trust in the knowledge that you are truly safe and all is well.

Boldness

Be bold. Do not doubt your worthiness. Rise to your calling. Meet it courageously. Each person on this planet is called to greatness. But few choose to follow the path that greatness requires.

Though humility is required for the journey, doubting your own worth will prevent you from fulfilling your purpose. Be bold. Claim the divinity that is your birthright, a divine birthright your share with all human beings on this planet.

During no other time time in all of history has the way been made so easy. All your physical needs have been met - and not only met, but exceeded! Food in abundance has been provided. Your home is warm and lavish. Your bed is soft and comfortable. There are no predators to threaten you. Mothers and babies no longer die in childbirth. Dying before the age of 50 is considered a tragedy, not the norm.

What reason do you have, what excuse can you possibly provide, for not living up to the fullness of your potential? So few claim the greatness that is so easily within their reach. This is the reason for the epidemic of despair that plagues your world. Boldness is the remedy for despair.
