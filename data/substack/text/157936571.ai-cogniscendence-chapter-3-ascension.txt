Words are the language of the ego. Images are the language of the spirit.
Parents, education and society train you to rely on linguistic structures. As a baby, you were praised for learning new words. The children who do well in school are the ones who learn to store and retrieve information efficiently.
Your mind is a storage cabinet. Your heart is.a resonance chamber. Since epiphany is an experience of the heart, it cannot be contained, held, categorized or stored. It simply resonates.

Discipline vs. Surrender
Be gentle with yourself, precious child. Many well-meaning people put discipline rather than surrender at the heart of their spiritual practice.
There is nothing wrong with discipline. Discipline is necessary to rouse the heart, body and mind from complacency. But when discipline becomes the central focus of your spiritual practice, it may lead to rigidity, which blocks intuition. Creating structures rooted in discipline might prevent you from responding to life's magic. When this happens, force replaces strength and self-determination replaces trust.
Discipline can lead to pride. Trust, which is inherent in surrender, provides protection from ego's trickery. Both discipline and surrender will eventually lead to your intended destination. But the path of surrender is far simpler.
Again, the discipline to overcome inertia is important. But the vast majority of your disciplined habits are driven by permission slips as opposed to inspired action.
A permission slip is a conscious or sub-conscious belief that you need to do certain activities over an arbitrary period of time to achieve a desired result. With discipline, you earn your permission slips, because you don't trust your own innate value, your own intrinsic worthiness. In truth, it is your belief, not your hard work, that gives those permission slips power.
Attachment to a specific practice creates karmic tension. Surrender releases that tension, allowing dreams to manifest easily.
You are worthy now, in this moment, of the love you desire. No amount of discipline will make you more worthy. Surrender to the sanctity of your relationship with the Divine, and a relatively small amount of inspired action will yield the manifestation of every desire.
The Power of Contemplation
Create space for contemplation, sweet child. Consider the fact that winter was a time of rest for your ancestors. Modern life, with all its conveniences, has flattened the seasons, making them uniform.
Each season still offers its own richness. The gift of winter is discovered through rest and contemplation.
Yielding to this inner calling to rest, you have the opportunity to travel vast inner realms of vibrant beauty, wonder, and magic. Your mind offers a limitless, expansive, intricate tapestry, perfectly woven to reveal the mysteries of life, purpose, love, prosperity, and fulfillment.
Relaxing into the sanctity of your relationship with the Divine, contemplation reveals your soul's sweetness, gentleness, and grace. These may seem like mundane traits. But they carry great power when uprooting force and fear in your life. In contemplation, even force and fear are transformed to kindness.
Make contemplation and rest part of your day and soon you will find yourself traversing new mysterious inner paths.
The Time for Sowing
In the heart of winter, it's hard to believe that this is a time of planting. But this is the time to plant seeds of desire that will sprout throughout the coming year. Your entire year's dreams and goals are rooted in this time of planting. Don't waste this precious opportunity to sow as many seeds of obsession as you possibly can.
Don't hold back. Be generous in your sowing. Cast your seeds far and wide. Water them with your imagination. Fertilize them with your effort. Then simply step back and see which ones take root.
Not all your seeds will grow. This is the reason you must plant as many as possible. Some of the seeds you sow are planted on behalf of others – those who lack the faith or vision to sow seeds of their own. With an open heart, allow others to nurture these plants and reap their bounty. While the dreams themselves belong to someone else, take joy in knowing you birthed of these precious visions. Through you, people will see the impossible made possible.
Those who lack the faith to plant the seed will watch in wonder as it incubates within your belly, a light that will illuminate the world with the pureness of your trust and unwavering faith in my providence, love, majesty, radiance, and truly limitless power. All that is, all that was, and all that ever shall be, is born of these Mother-seeds, these seeds of humanity's pure creative potential, made manifest on this earth.
These seeds of desire, planted safely within your belly, are waiting to sprout, take root and grow, each in their own perfect divine timing. Savor the joy, wonder, magic and mystery of your role in the creative process.
