Gene Key 24: Addiction-Invention-Silence
Human Design Gate 24: Rationalization

Addiction is distraction from purposeless, emptiness, and boredom. As each of these fears ultimately represents the fear of death, addiction reflects a deep, visceral need to feel alive.
Do not feel shame for your addiction. It is your greatest teacher. Whether you are addicted to drugs, alcohol, sex, work, shopping, the internet, video games, food or gambling, your addiction will not release its hold until you learn the lesson it’s here to teach you.
Rather than trying to resist, battle, or overcome addiction, try approaching it with inquisitiveness and curiosity. Poke at the edges of your fear. Explore what boredom feels like.
With awe and wonder, you will discover that silence isn’t empty. It’s vibrant, alive, and pregnant with possibility. Once you embrace discomfort rather than trying to escape it, addiction will no longer have a reason to stay.
Relax Into the Space Between
Sometimes kindness means giving space. Sending love through prayer, offering loving intentions, and holding someone in your thoughts can be more effective than words or physical touch.
In the space between moments of connection, silence provides room to create a shift. Set your intentions for the highest and best outcome in a difficult situation, both for yourself and your loved ones.
Hold a vision of peace, joy, wonder, surprise and delight. Know that you are held, and that all beings on your planet are similarly held, in the hands of a loving Mother who is leveraging universal forces on your behalf.
Just by setting an intention for health and healing, you are calling on spiritual allies who know how to most efficiently and effectively respond to that intention.
Relax into the space between, precious soul. Relax into this wondrous power – this quiet energy of not doing. Allow the expansiveness of imagination to create the most beneficial outcome in this situation, one that elicits pure joy and delight.
Momentary loneliness and even pain are a small price to pay for the beautiful love that awaits you on the other side of the space between.
Relax into the pause. Relax into the silence. Allow the darkness to surround you in a blanket of warmth. Like a seed lying dormant in the earth, love is waiting for the perfect moment to take root and grow. But for now, rest and relax into the space between.
Move Forward Slowly
Emerging from darkness is like waking up after a deep sleep. It can be a little disorienting. Do not feel pressured to do anything right now. Just relax into this state of being.
Explore the many ways you can simply be. Don't try to figure out your next steps or a path forward. Simply relax into silence until you have your balance.
Even when you regain your footing, move forward slowly and with simplicity. Remember to smile whenever possible. Be gentle with yourself and others. Practice pliability. There is no sense of urgency.
Reject judgment in any form. Simply refuse to perceive anything as judgement. You are all kids at the playground. Even if you hurt or offend one of your playmates with your words or actions, if you avoid engaging, they will forget it and you will be able to play with them again later. Offer a sincere apology and let it sit.
Give gifts with an open heart. Allow love to guide your path and be wide open to the leadings of your soul.
Quiet Your Mind
I can be found in the quiet places of your heart, dear one. For this reason, it is so important to rest. In the stillness of your soul, in your body's perfect silence, words disappear as they are replaced by impressions of love.
Words are the language of the ego. Images are the language of the spirit. As you quiet your mind, gently releasing all words, you will feel my presence, the presence of the Mother, the one who nurtures you, tends to your heart, and makes you whole in that delicious space between breaths.
Feel your lungs expand with divine grace, breathing the light of my love deep into your soul. Feel love, warmth, compassion, and illumination fill your being with all that you thought was missing. In truth, it was never really gone.
Though you momentarily allow this world to distract you from the Is-ness of All-That-Is, inner truth and inner knowing are evidence of my love, and my love can never actually leave you.
Open and allow your heart to return to that inner place of knowing. Peace, harmony, laughter and love will be right there where they've been all along, waiting to greet you with open arms.
All is well, precious one. Relax into the fullness, the joy of All-That-Is.
