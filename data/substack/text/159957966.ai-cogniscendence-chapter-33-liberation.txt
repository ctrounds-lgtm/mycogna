The Power of Surrender
Surrender isn't about giving up. It's about letting go of external validation. Surrender provides the shift in perspective that helps identify what you truly want – the thing that truly matters.
Maybe you've dreamed of having your own business. Surrender may be accepting a job that provides
freedom
and
abundance
. Maybe you've dreamed of finding the perfect life partner. Surrender may be discovering
laughter
and
love
in the intimacy and companionship of a few dear friends.
In surrendering, you maintain authorship while releasing attachment to a specific outcome. Relax into the knowing that all is well. There is joy in the discovery that nothing is standing in the way of your bliss. Nothing is preventing you from feeling perfect peace. Right now, in this moment, you have full access to all the happiness you desire.
It's actually funny when you think about it. You expend so much energy building castles in the sky. Your aspirations are founded on the premise that you need to expend time, effort, and sacrifice to achieve the object of your desire. In reality, peace, love, and freedom, which are the intended outcome of those desires, come from just letting go.
All of your life's greatest achievements have no more permanence than the clouds that float across the sky. Peace and well-being are the only lasting measure of success. In any given moment, you have the power to break free from expectations tied to external validation and claim your role as creator.
Expansiveness
The expansiveness of life is so wonderful. Would you agree? If your life feels small – if you are resigned to lack and limitation in any aspect of your life – you have not stepped into the fullness of who you are.
There is no such thing as an inspiration you are not fully worthy and capable of fulfilling. Your power to create is virtually limitless. If there is a desire in your heart, you are strong enough, smart enough, talented enough and abundant enough to achieve it. All is within your reach. All is achievable with the leverage of Universal forces, conspiring to work on your behalf to bring about that which you have birthed into form through your desire.
The object of your desire already exists. It was fully formed in the non-physical when you first conceived of it. Bringing it forth into the physical realm is only a matter of bridging the gap between wishing and knowing. Once you truly understand this, once you truly learn just how simple manifestation can be, you can apply this formula to all aspects of your life.
Choose something easy; an area of your life where self-limiting beliefs are the weakest. Feel the collaborative powers of the Universe working in you and through you to easily and effortlessly manifest a desire. It may seem so easy, so commonplace, that you've forgotten there was a time when this form of manifestation was not second nature. Feel the magic in the ease with which you accomplish something you once found difficult.
Appreciate your power as an expression of the Creator, playing in this aspect of your life that feels so expansive, joyous, and creative. Celebrate the expansiveness of your creativity, allowing the energy of abundance to flow into all other areas of your life as well.
Your life is meant to be sweet – filled with vibrancy, vitality, and bliss.
Overcoming Challenges
The challenges in your life are gifts, specifically chosen for you, to teach you something important that you might otherwise have missed. The nature of the challenge is not important. It may have to do with relationships, finances, career, or health. All challenges have the same underlying energetic function: to honor your soul's agreement to evolve.
Many challenges are rooted in the need for control. The more you release control, allowing your soul to embody this physical form and do the heavy lifting for you, the faster and more easily you can overcome frustration and dis-ease.
Many challenges are rooted in lack of self-worth. Connecting with your higher self - embodying your soul - allows you to view yourself from a new perspective: one of love, light, and blessedness. There is grace all around you!
Guilt, doubt, jealousy, denial, lack and limitation are not serving you. They steal your power. They are at the root of your stuck-ness.
Release the negative energies impeding your progress. It often easier than you think. Have the courage to dive deep into fear. Open your heart and allow my strength to help process the feelings you are afraid to feel. As fear is identified and felt, it is released. Then the fear itself will be transmuted to love, healing your heart, mind, and spirit as it flows through you in a river of light.
