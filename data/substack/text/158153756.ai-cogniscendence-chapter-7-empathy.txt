Empathy
February 2-7
Gene Key 13: Discord-Discernment-Empathy
Human Design Gate 13: Listener
I Ching Hexagram 13: 同人 (tóng rén), “Fellowship with People”
If you truly believe that everything happens for a reason, you recognize that each person and event in your life is a precious gift, specially selected just for you by a loving Mother.
Sometimes, the very best gifts are disguised as challenges, requiring different skills to unwrap them. Though they may not seem like gifts at first, time and insight will reveal tools that allow you to open the gift with your heart instead of your mind.
One of those tools is empathy. No matter how hard your own life circumstance may be, there is always someone else who is experiencing something worse. Use challenge as a compass, directing you to others who need inspiration to overcome a similar challenge. Unified in a common purpose, you will find the strength to overcome any difficulty.
Kindness is a Beacon
You can't be sad enough to make a sad person happy. You can't be sick enough to make a sick person well. You can't be lost enough to help a lost person find their way. Kindness does not require you to share in their sadness, their sickness or their confusion. Kindness requires you to stand as a beacon, guiding them home.
Your kindness provides a safe haven, sheltering your loved ones from the storm. This requires your strength, not your weakness. Standing firm in your joy, your happiness, your place of perfect peace, is the truest measure of kindness.
This may feel awkward. Many mistakenly believe that empathy means sharing in pain or misery. This couldn't be further from the truth. Two miserable people don't make a happy person.
Gently tell your loved one that you are dedicating this moment of happiness to them. Let them know that you are honoring their sadness by surrounding them in a warm blanket of love and joy. It's your loving presence, not your shared sadness, that has the power to truly heal them.
Humans have the tendency to match frequencies. The lower vibration should always attune to the higher vibration, not the other way around. That is the only way to raise the vibrational frequency on the planet throughout humanity as a whole.
Your Highest Purpose
Allow the Universe to act on your behalf. To do this, you need to let go of your agenda. Let go of your need to control the outcome. Let go of your desire to map out a strategy, develop a plan, or set expectations as to how your future will unfold.
Stop trying to use force to achieve your desires. Your discipline is nothing in comparison with the leverage at your disposal when you access my power. There is freedom in discovering that your life was actually meant to be easy.
Throw away the measuring stick you are using to measure yourself. It was never yours anyway. The belief that blood, sweat, tears, and pain are the price you need to pay for freedom is silly. That perspective isn't in alignment with who you are and it's tricked you into believing that your life's purpose is connected to doing and having, rather than being.
Kindness is the ability to stop that nonsense and lean into the things that are most important. They are right in front of you. Don't drop the ball. Don't fail to see the people who need you, who bask in the radiance of your love. There is no agenda so important that it is a worthy trade for kindness.
Right now, go to those you've forgotten, those whose needs you've missed due to busyness, self-absorption or distraction. Take them in your arms and tell them you are sorry. Promise that you will be there for them. Let them know they are your priority.
Don't miss this opportunity to allow your loved ones to see your vulnerability. Your humility itself is an act of kindness.
Then offer yourself grace. Forgive yourself for your momentary lapse of kindness. It's all part of the greater journey and this too is part of the plan. All has been divinely orchestrated for your own greater good and the greater good of those around you.
There is no higher mission than that of attending to your loved ones. All agendas are subservient to this greater mission, this higher plan.
Identify Your Narratives
Let your loved ones know you cherish them. Take every opportunity to let them know your deep love for them. Surprise them with their favorite foods!
There are many things you may incorrectly assume about those who are closest to you because your narratives have been reinforced, over and over. People have the tendency to accept information that fits their narrative, while discarding information that does not.
What if you pretend you are meeting your parents, your spouse, your children or your siblings for the very first time? What questions would you ask them over coffee? What are they most proud of? What do they wish they'd done differently? The answers might surprise you.
The image of your loved one is simply a construction in your mind - a story you've told yourself about who they are.
Only your soul, your higher self, knows the truth of who they really are. Ask your soul to reveal the essence of your loved one to you today, and find your relationship growing in ways you would have never thought possible.
