Complexity is the tendency to reject simple solutions. You believe that an easy answer can’t possibly be right. An overabundance of data leads to confusion. Fear of making a mistake creates a state of paralysis where decisions are avoided or postponed.
Nature provides the perfect model for simplicity. Though nature is complex, it isn’t complicated. Nature thrives when conditions are balanced.
The Golden Rule is an excellent example of a simple formula that can be applied to complex situations. “Do onto others as you would have done onto you.”
At its quintessence, the Golden Rule answers the question, “What does love look like in this moment?”
Love of a Lifetime
Are you ready for the love of a lifetime? Be prepared. It will look nothing like you expect it to look.
Many people confuse possession with love. Contrary to popular belief, the institution of marriage is rooted in possession, not love. In fact, the concept of marrying for love is a relatively new experiment.
Terms like "committed relationship" or "life-partner" also infer exclusivity. This is not love in the truest sense of the word as exclusivity rarely serves the exacting demands of love.
True love seeks that which is in the highest and best good, not only for the individual, but for all humanity. In this way, true love is freedom from the need to possess or be possessed.
The concept of finding "the one" represents a grave misunderstanding of what true love looks like. In true love, "the one" is found within the many. This does not imply promiscuity, as intimacy and sex do not necessarily go hand-in-hand. In fact, its much healthier to view sex as a biological function as opposed to a measure of intimacy.
Love can be described as the rapture that comes from holding space for another person's joy. As the atom holds an enormous amount of space when compared to the Infinitesimally small particles within it, so your own love is virtually boundless when compared with the conditions needed to inspire it.
Celebrate love as it emerges in its many forms. Make way for new expressions of love that look nothing like you've experienced before. Unconditionally support humanity as it explores what it means to be truly alive in these bodies. Revel in the miraculous splendor of the human form. Refrain from judgement and never withhold your love.
An open heart will find love in unlikely places. Surrender to the intoxicating scent of pleasure, as it is a mirrored reflection of the exquisite perfection of nature. You – an infinite being in physical form – are nothing less than an incarnate manifestation of the Divine, expressing into this lifetime for the sole purpose of touching the earth and touching man's heart.
Reach for the light now kindled inside of you. Be fierce and unwavering in your commitment to embody inner wisdom. Unlock that which has been hiding within the structure of human DNA for many millennia. This is the time of the great arising, the dawn of a new earth that will look nothing like the old one.
This is the love of a lifetime, resonating throughout the planet in fractal patterns, as it is repeated, magnified, and amplified in a resounding chorus of light and love so strong, nothing can stand against it.
What will you do today to amplify that love? How bravely will you sing your heart-song? What song will you inspire in the hearts of others who even now are choosing to join the chorus?
This, sweet child, is the love of a lifetime. And you are indeed worthy of being in love.
Desire is Like Mental Chewing Gum
What do you need to believe in? What is the gift you are patiently (or not so patiently) waiting to receive? What is the longing that touches the tender part of your heart? Maybe the longing is soft like a gentle tug. Maybe it's more acute, like an ache. How would the experience of wanting shift if you smiled when you thought of it? And how would it shift if you laughed?
Even the deepest ache, the most powerful human longing is nothing more than discomfort. Things aren't nearly as serious as you make them out to be. You are all just children playing on a playground. And events are unfolding exactly as they are meant to unfold. Your every desire is heard and known. Your every need, your every want, your every wish is precious beyond measure.
While it's true that your wanting is not serious, it is important. But the discomfort associated with wanting is unnecessary.
Discomfort comes from focusing on the past or the future. Maybe the object of your desire is something you've wanted for a long time and you've become worried it might never arrive.
Can you find peace in this moment? Right now as you read these words, can you find peace in the wanting itself?
If it wasn't this, it would be something else. After all, desire is like mental chewing gum. It gives your mind something to do. The mind will always find something to want. The magic is to find peace in the wanting, whether or not the object of your desire takes shape in physical form.
Relax into the mystery of this playground and just let your soul do what it does best... play!
Leverage Collaborative Forces
In the "doing", there will always be more to do. It's in the "being" that your true power lies. In doing, you are limited by time and circumstances. In being, your creative power is limitless.
In your mind's eye, imagine your perfect outcome. Do you want a beautiful, healthy, perfectly toned body? Do you want an exciting new career that provides the flexibility to travel the world? Do you want to find the love of your life?
Holding an image of the perfect outcome in your mind, feel excitement and gratitude for the dreams that are making their way towards you, into this physical experience, right now. Collaborative forces are conspiring on your behalf. Tune into your soul's voice, inspiring you to take specific action steps, which will provide the quickest, most expedient path to your goals.
Act on that inner intuition immediately and without question. Those gentle inner leadings have been inspired as a direct response to your asking. These are the baby steps that make your dreams manifest in this physical realm.
Lengthy to-do lists are not only counterproductive, needlessly consuming precious time and energy, they may also lead to frustration and disappointment. Leverage the co-creative partnerships available to you in non-physical realms, and act on the guidance they provide.
Take joy in these baby steps, knowing that you are not meant to achieve your dreams alone. Your dreams are birthed by your soul. They are your unique and valuable contribution to the world, providing your truest sense of purpose in this life experience.
