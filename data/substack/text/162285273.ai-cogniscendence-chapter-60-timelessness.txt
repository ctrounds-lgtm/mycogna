Timelessness
December 4-8
Gene Key 5:
Impatience-Patience-Timelessness
Human Design Gate 5:
Patterns
I Ching Hexagram 5:
需 (xū), “Waiting”
The time between the inception of a desire and its fruition can seem endless. As time passes, you begin to doubt your worthiness. You wonder if your actions are misguided or if there’s something important you are missing.
Patience is the unwavering trust in divine timing. Looking to nature for inspiration, you see the perfection in nature’s rhythms and cycles. All creation is growing, dying, and being born anew. Why should dreams be any different?
Obsessing over desires, you miss life’s radiant beauty. You miss that sweet expansiveness where moments morph into lifetimes. Timelessness is a complete surrender to the moment, the only place that offers perfect freedom. Perfection lies in the space between heartbeats.
Surrender to Timelessness
Stop resisting happiness. Stop making excuses as to why your life should be anything but pure bliss. Stop believing you need to earn your success or achievements through the expenditure of time, energy and money. Stop thinking it has to be hard. Relax. Enjoy. Play. Savor the ease with which your life unfolds, virtually effortlessly, calling all that you desire into physical form.
There is a time for sowing seeds. There is a time for tending to your garden — feeding your dreams and watering them. But you put far too much emphasis on doing rather than just being. Attend to your own precious heart. Allow your dreams to transform you.
Spend time in contemplation. Relax into the gentle unfolding in the space between the inception of a desire and its fruition, allowing perfect timing to dictate the span in which your dreams unfold. There is no need to rush or press or push through. You will only create needless stress and prolong the fruition of your desire by unintentionally counter-creating its opposite through doubt and worry.
Pressure and expectations, attaching to man’s timing instead of divine timing, impedes the Universe’s ability to act expediently. Release. Let go. Do only the daily work that is being impressed upon your heart. Then stop, even if that means only doing “productive activity” a few minutes a day. Do that and nothing more. As you surrender to timelessness, your faith creates space for mountains to move on your behalf.
It often helps to take your gaze from your own desires to focus on assisting someone else in the manifestation of their dreams. Act boldly, with kindness and generosity of spirit, and you will inspire the Universe to act boldly, in kindness and generosity on your behalf.
Divine Timing
Divine timing is a fulcrum that provides leverage to quickly and easily manifest all your deepest desires. You are meant to have each and every one of your soul’s desires. You are not meant to struggle and expend needless effort in achieving your goals. Save that life-force energy for your rich, beautiful relationships with others and with the Divine.
Surrender to the spirit of timelessness as your dreams are made manifest in this physical realm. There is great love for you in the non-physical — many helpers and guides who are even now orchestrating events on your behalf to bring about your highest and greatest good.
Take a pause. Look around and be aware of the people and circumstances that have been put in your path at precisely the right moment to bring about the fruition of all you hope to manifest. Your success is assured, dear one. The path has already been laid and your way is clear. Simply open and allow the unfolding of that which is now easily and effortlessly finding its way to you.
There is no lack or limitation, only joy and abundance. Play, dear soul. Play. Relax into the timeless space between the inception of a desire and its fruition, as you welcome the assistance of divine timing.
Your Creative Power is in Your Present
Endings are just new beginnings. Don’t be sad when something ends. It simply leaves space for evolution, new creation, and a pathway to things far greater than what you left behind.
All your creative power is in your present. Your past holds no creative energy. Focus your sight on that which you hope to manifest in the future and your days will be filled with joyous expectation.
You deserve all the love, all the richness, all the pleasure working its way to you in this very moment. Indeed, the entire universe is conspiring on your behalf to bring that which is of your highest and greatest good, dear one.
There is no such thing as a mistake. All events have been transpiring exactly as they were meant to transpire. All previous lovers and relationships were specially selected to teach you, hone you, shape you, mold you into the person who is truly worthy of the expansive, joyous love now manifesting in your life.
Yours is a path of richness and delight. Yours is a path of blessing and honor. Celebrate, dear one, this beautiful life you are living, as creator in your own divine creation.
