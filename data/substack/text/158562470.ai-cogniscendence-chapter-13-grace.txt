The heart closes when a person feels they are undeserving of love or beyond forgiveness.
Grace has a domino affect. Acknowledging and accepting your own past failures makes you more compassionate and understanding when loved ones falter. In receiving your grace, your loved ones are more likely to offer grace to others.
Responding with kindness in the face of pain transforms your wounds into a source of strength. You stand as a beacon of hope for those who need to know that no one is beyond redemption.
Through grace, we discover that every emotion – be it joyful or painful – is truly an expression of divine love.
Grace
Grace is the kindness you offer yourself. When you're feeling sad, lonely or out of sorts, have the grace to pause for reflection and ask, "What does my soul want me to know?" Maybe you need to rest. Maybe you need to go for a hike or a run. Maybe you need to be immersed in nature.
In some cases, offering grace means diving into a project that is calling to you – something that leverages your natural creativity and talent. And sometimes grace means spending time with the people you love.
Attend now to the needs of your soul. Attend to the truth of who you are apart from your meetings and to-do lists. Relaxing into that higher perspective, allow your jaw to relax. Feel the space beneath your eyes open and expand. Take a deep breath in through your nose, and on the exhale, feel that relaxation drop down through your shoulders, your chest and your belly. Allow the space between your ears to expand.
Sit for a moment in stillness, attending to the sounds around you. Do you hear a faint humming? Fully relaxed, you can actually hear the vibrational energy field around your body, supporting you, holding you, permeating each cell.
From this infinite field of energy, draw all the resources you need right now. An infinite supply of vibrance, vitality, passion, excitement, enthusiasm and adventure await you in this very moment.
Step into the richness of your full creativity. Allow it to express in your life now. All abundance and wealth is available to you when you access the wellspring of your creative power.
Today, allow grace, not your to-do list, to dictate the rhythm of your day, and watch as pure magic unfolds.
Release Control
As counterintuitive as it sounds, your power lies in your ability to consistently, willingly and easily release control.
Relax into the space between your humanity and your divinity. View body and mind from your soul's perspective. Release attachments to the arbitrary life circumstances that form the foundation of your social validation. Achievements, beauty, athleticism, social standing; all of these rest on nothing more than the shifting sands of your humanity, not the solid rock foundation of your divinity. Shifting sands are easily moved or even lost when life's river surges or changes course.
Forgiveness. Compassion. Love. Intimacy. These are expressions of your divinity. These are the characteristics that will show up in your life when you choose to embody your soul. Release control, letting go of the ego-mind's attachment to validation in the form of achievement, beauty, and success.
Free of these distractions, step into the river's powerful current to create something so much greater, so much more permanent than anything you could possibly build on sand. Your very presence will change the course of the river, transforming not only your life, but the lives of thousands.
Find the courage to leave the water's edge. Surrendering to the river's flow, your life will unfold in miraculous ways you can't even begin to imagine.
Surrender to Grace
Open and allow. The seat of consciousness is available in the very moment you choose to claim it. Unending love, abounding joy, profound peace, deep understanding, all the spiritual conditions you are trying to achieve in your life experience here on earth are so easily and readily available to you right now, in this moment.
You need only rest, relax, be at peace, drink in the silence, and open to your natural state of being, which is love. There is nothing you need to be or do to be worthy of this love. You are worthy right now, in this moment. You are everything you were ever meant to be, dear child. You already are.
Life is unfolding, in this very moment, in its greatest expression of joy and beauty. It is only your erroneous perception of lack and limitation that prevents you from seeing the divine perfection of it all.
Release control. Surrender to the light and love of All-That-Is. You are so very precious. You are so very loved. Surrender to the grace that is all around you now.
