Inadequacy is the nagging suspicion that you’re just not enough: not smart enough, not attractive enough, not rich enough, not brave enough, not strong enough. It is the pervasive feeling that something important is missing.
In addition to the great abundance of talents and abilities you possess, there is a virtually infinite well of collective universal resources at your disposal. This well contains all the strength, creativity, ingenuity, and vision you need to accomplish any dream that inspires you.
Wisdom is the recognition that through the lens of divine perfection, it’s impossible to be anything but perfect. With a playful, childlike spirit, you recognize that your life’s purpose has already been fulfilled through your soul’s incarnation on this planet at this time.
Wisdom
The feeling of inadequacy is a ubiquitous human experience.
You were born with a unique set of gifts. It may surprise you to learn that each person receives gifts in equal measure. Your perception of inequality is partially due to the fact that your culture assigns more weight to some gifts than others. Another reason for the perceived inequality is that gifts can be disguised as challenges. To further complicate matters, many people fail to claim their gifts due to feelings of inadequacy or hide gifts that might make them appear weak. But in truth, if gifts were assigned an atomic weight, each person's gifts would balance any other.
In Western culture, intellectual intelligence has historically been prioritized over emotional intelligence. Though both men and women share both types of intelligence, intellectual intelligence is considered a masculine energy while emotional intelligence is considered a feminine energy. When you look at familial, organizational and governmental structures of power and control, the predominance of men in positions of power is indicative of the supposition that knowledge is more favorable than intuition.
Acquisition of knowledge provides a false sense of security. Most people feel deeply uncomfortable with the unknown. Focusing on subjects that are quantifiable and measurable provides the illusion of control. Education provides instruction on that which is knowable (science) while systemically avoiding that which is unknowable (spirit). This does little to alleviate fear, since fear stems from the unknown, not the known. Paradoxically, the more science learns, the less it understands.
True wisdom, and your source of security, lies in your ability to trust. Wisdom abandons the need to know. In trust, you embody the quiet / alert perspective of an infant. You approach the world with curiosity and inquisitiveness. Inadequacy dissolves when you empty your mind and boldly surrender to the mystery of the void.
Ancient Chinese teachers refer to this infinite source of wisdom as "the well". This is the well that holds all your fears, and all your gifts. When you have the courage to draw from this well, you discover your deepest fears are actually your most profound gifts in disguise. Inadequacy is nothing more than the arrow pointing the way to those gifts, which were specially chosen just for you by a loving Mother. Surrendering to the Great Mystery, you are finally able to live a life of freedom, as fear no longer has power over you.
You are perfect, whole and complete, exactly as you are. And so is every other human being on your planet. You are playing the perfect role you intended to play when you chose to embark on this intriguing life adventure.
Retroactive Gratitude
Isn't it funny how gratitude becomes easier with practice? The simple act of smiling is an act of gratitude, an expression of happiness.
When you smile for no reason, your subconscious will look for reasons to be happy. And voila! Instant gratitude!
Feel blessed. Become aware of the blessings all around you that are indeed precious gifts just waiting to be unwrapped. In sacred ceremony, you discover these gifts were there all along, waiting for you to claim them.
There is no expiration date! You can claim those gifts in your memory. So even if you didn't feel appreciation for them at the time you experienced the blessing, you can reach back into your memory and feel appreciation for blessings received in the past.
Focus on one small topic, then allow gratitude to emerge as you explore the many blessings surrounding that one topic. Through this simple practice, you can access an endless source of gratitude and appreciation as you review the abundance of blessings you've received in this lifetime.
Open and Allow
Trust that all is well. Keep an open mind. An empty vessel is easier to fill than one that is already full.
The years you've spent wrestling with questions about life's purpose amount to little more than mental chewing gum. The knowledge is virtually worthless as it will die with you. The true value of contemplation lies in the way it works on your heart, transforming your state of being.
Do you believe you have surpassed any other human being in any way? These thoughts stem from arrogance. In truth, most spiritual pursuits are arrogance in disguise. The temptation to share wisdom is evidence of an underlying need for external validation.
Allow wisdom to flow through your fingers. Resist the urge to grasp and hold it. Like the river's gentle flow, wisdom passes in and out of your mind when you allow grace to saturate your heart. Only then will it change your being-ness.
This is the time when all will be made clear and you will revel in the joyous perfection of all that is unfolding. You simply need to trust. Open and allow the transformation to happen. Do not press or push. Allow its unfolding to occur gently and easily, with simplicity and grace.
