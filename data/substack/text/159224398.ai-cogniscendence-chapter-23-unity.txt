Soul Embodiment
What does it mean to embody your soul? Embodiment is a spiritual state, where you give your body over to the highest part of you – the part that is the Mother's energy, manifest in you, in this physical form. My energy is your spiritual lifeblood. It is the part of you that existed before you took form in this physical body. It will exist after you die. And it is present in every moment of your life, closer than your own skin.
In moments of sadness, anger, loneliness, and pain, your conscious mind feels separated from my love, which lives inside your soul. But this separation is an illusion, because my love never leaves you. It is inextricably bound to your essence, unified with your soul.
When you feel joy, rapture, bliss, and love, your conscious mind is in alignment with your soul. Embodiment is the process of consciously tuning into this alignment, opening and allowing your own soul to allow my love – Mother love – to pour through you. This is accomplished by relaxing your mind, releasing control, surrendering the driver's seat to your soul, and allowing intuition to take the wheel.
Seeing the world through the eyes of your soul, hearing as your soul hears, feeling, tasting, touching, sensing this world with the help of your soul, you will become aware of an indescribable unification with Mother love, my love, that has been waiting to greet you since the day your were born.
Perfect Unity
Perfect unity. This is your heart's desire, its quintessential craving. There is no desire stronger than the heart's longing to be fully and completely united.
Precious child, that love is so much closer than you think. In fact, it has been right in front of you all along. Your own soul is the one you seek. Your quest for union is the desire to see your own soul reflected in the eyes of another.
Instead of trying to convince others to fall in love with you for who you're not, feel your soul, falling in love with you over and over again, for who you are. Your soul sees the fullness of your perfection as a fully actualized being, worthy of union with the divine. You are an expression of the Creator living in his or her own creation.
Embody your soul, allowing it to fill every aspect of your being. Feel the spontaneous, joyful energy that inspires you to play. Listen to your soul whispering encouragement to breathe deeply, laugh, sing, dance and smile.
Challenge yourself to smile twice as much today as you did yesterday. Then smile twice as much tomorrow as you did today.
There is no limit to the joy you are capable of experiencing. Your own self-worth is the only limiting factor. Expand your self-worth and your joy expands accordingly, because you are truly worthy of bliss; physical, emotional, and spiritual ecstasy. Claim these gifts by embodying your soul. Rest assured. The love you seek is already yours.
