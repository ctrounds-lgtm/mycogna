Humility
Arrogance exists in almost every archetype. Most of the shadow frequencies are obsessed with the word "I". This exaggerated sense of self creates the illusion that your perspective is more correct than anyone else's.
Consider this. Is there anyone else whose perspective is more accurate than yours? Is there anyone who is more "right" than you are? As perspectives are affected by a multitude of factors, including culture, conditioning, and life experiences, they will obviously be different for each person. Nevertheless, most of you believe that the extent to which another person aligns with your views and values is the extent to which they are right. The extent to which another person does not align with your views and values is the extent to which they are wrong.
At the deeper shadow frequencies, arrogance makes you entirely unaware of the needs or perspectives of others. Undercurrents of narcissism desensitize you to global issues like world hunger, climate change, poverty, and war. Though large corporations and the mega-rich are condemned for self-serving agendas, the fear-based need to hoard resources for "me and mine" is found at all socio-economic levels.
That having been said, there are leaders who understand that a proposition where "we" win and "they" lose is not an acceptable solution. True leaders take a broader view, one that encompasses not only humanity, but nature and the planet as well. These leaders live by the phrases "Do onto others as you would have others do onto you," and "But for the grace of God, there go I."
Humility is the mental flexibility that recognizes your perspective is no more right, accurate or correct than anyone else's. Humility understands that there is no such thing as moral superiority. It does not use trickery to manipulate or make excuses for self-centric behavior. Humility is the recognition that a person's lot in life does not determine his / her worth. Likewise, achievement, intellect and power are not a measure of value. Humility is the recognition that divine sovereignty lives within each person in equal measure.
From the Mother's perspective, the needs of one are the needs of all.
What is Alive Within You?
What is alive within you? So much of your day is occupied with thoughts and activities that do not feed you. Instead, they leave you feeling spiritually and emotionally starved. So then, what is alive in you? What causes your heart to skip a beat when you think of it? What soothes your mind when you relax into it? If nothing comes to mind, you need to rest.
In the delicious, sweet solitude of contemplation, you will find that which is truly alive in you. Set aside time when you first wake in the morning. Lay in your bed and savor the expansive creativity of your mind. Set your alarm an hour early so you have time to honor the sacred space between sleep and waking. This is the time your intuition is most alive. This this is the time your dreams – both sleeping and waking – are most vibrant.
Are you one who feels despair in the early morning? Don't be concerned. Commit to waking an hour before rising, and you will find your despair magically transforms to hope.
This is the time of spiritual power, where the voice of Spirit is louder than the voice of ego. Harness the power of this waking hour. Feel the sensual tingle of connection as your angels and spirit guides offer insight, intuition and direction.
Before going to bed at night, ask your questions. Ask for clarity. Ask for evidence that you are loved and worthy of your most cherished desires. When you wake in the morning, listen. Maintain a wordless space. Words are the language of ego. Images are the language of Spirit. In deep humility, occupy this wordless space, opening your heart and intuition to the life-giving images your soul is waiting to offer.
The Need for Touch
Humans are meant to be touched. They are meant to be held. The sensation of touch, as well as all you perceive with your physical senses, is what sets you apart from all that exists in the realms of the non-physical.
Touch is indeed one of the sensations you were most looking forward to experiencing when you made the decision to embark on this adventure in time and space.
Offer hugs freely. Don't be afraid to touch those who may appear to be untouchable. When you are unable to touch with your hands, reach out with your eyes, allowing the warmth of your smile to convey love.
Do not allow your ego to inhibit this most honest expression of love. You are an expression of the Mother's energy in human form, a physical extension of her loving arms.
Offer compassion from a place of deep humility. Feel your way into the place of inner knowing, intuitively meeting the needs of others as they are placed on your heart.
Act boldly in love, and love will respond by acting boldly on your behalf, delivering to you all that your heart desires.
