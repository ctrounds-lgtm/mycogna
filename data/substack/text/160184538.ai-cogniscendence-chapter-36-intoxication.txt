Distraction is a form of pain avoidance. Social media, television, video games, or other recreational pursuits are part of a healthy work-life balance. But when these activities are used to avoid discomfort, they become demotivating.
Even pursuits such as work, physical fitness, and spiritual growth can deplete rather than enrich your life when they are used as a form of escape.
Enriching activities are ones that contribute to emotional wholeness and a sustained sense of well-being. Introspection can be painful. But the process of contemplation and integration yields new insights. Through self-discovery, you recover fragmented aspects of your truest, most authentic self.
Intoxication is the rapture of epiphany. You welcome the exquisite beauty of the pain as it yields fresh new insight.
Limitless Love
In contemplating the nature of life's truest purpose, you will understand that this purpose is one that applies to every human being. Your truest purpose is simple, though embodying it confounds even the greatest sages.
Life's purpose is to love. Love me, your Mother. Love the earth. Love each other. Love yourself. Every desire apart from this is just distraction.
Even worthy pursuits like world peace, ending hunger, or being a better person, are aspirations that can draw your attention from life's primary purpose. Though each of these pursuits may be rooted in love, they can easily become entangled in complexities that draw your focus from the love that inspired them. True freedom lies in releasing attachment to any vision apart from love.
Any human being on earth is capable of choosing love. Every person who has ever walked the earth was capable of choosing love. Every person who follows us into the future will be capable of choosing love.
Love, life's truest purpose, is within reach of every human being who suffers the ravages of war, hunger, pain, illness, and deep emotional trauma. Love is available to the poorest man on earth as well as the wealthiest. Love is available to those who are weak as well as those who are physically strong. Love is available to those with limited mental capabilities as well as those with great genius. Love is available to those who spend their entire lives in one place as well as those who travel the globe. Love is available to those who choose a simple life as well as those with great aspirations. Love is available to the spiritual pauper as well as the guru.
Life's truest purpose, for every human being without exception, is to love nature, Creator, self and other.
The amazing myriad of forms love takes as it expresses into this world is truly wondrous to contemplate. Your expansiveness in living out your life's purpose is virtually limitless. When you tap into your boundless creativity and become fully aware of the many ways you can express love in this lifetime, there is literally nothing you cannot accomplish.
Surrender to Nature
Nature: soft, soothing, sweet, gentle, silent. Nature: violent, immovable, unrelenting, turbulent, wild. Nature is not only a perfect metaphor for the diverse experiences of this human life, it is the catalyst to synthesize all these diverse aspects of human nature... or nature within the human.
Surrender to nature – outside and within. Surrender to the chaotic, sometimes messy perfection that defies control. Surrender to the beauty and majesty of the storm.
When the wind howls, the waves pound, the rain comes down in torrents, just laugh. Stand in the midst of it, and let the laughter roll through you. Dig your toes deep into the muddy earth. Savor the salty taste in your mouth as the purity of the rain mixes with your own precious tears; tears of pain, tears of loneliness, tears of anger and tears of joy. Yes, joy. Because finally, you recognize that the pain, loneliness, and anger are precious gifts from a loving Mother.
The storm is real. And so is the joy. They are intimately intertwined, like lovers madly striving to express the height and width and depth of their passion. Feel the caress of the storm through its fury. Feel the tender kiss of the divine through your pain. The pain itself is its own reward. In it, you are blessed beyond measure. In it, you are celebrated by all the heavenly host, all the spirits in heaven and on earth, who stand in awe and wonder at your strength, your courage, your beauty and your magnificence.
Love Is To Be Celebrated
Love, in all its forms, is to be celebrated. Is there love in your heart? If so, it is no mistake. There is no love, in any form, that is an error. Love has a transformative energy. It accomplishes miracles. It is not necessary for love to be returned in order for your love to give you wings. The power of flight is yours, just for the pure experience of loving.
Continue to draw strength and vitality from your love. Nurture it. Feed it. Allow it to grow. Never doubt it, question it, or try to put undue emphasis on having that love met with love in return. The truest form of love is that which requires nothing in return – not even to be loved back.
To fall in love is a precious gift, one that is received simply by opening and allowing the love to flow through you. Allow passion to bring a smile to your face and feel your vibration shift as your energy multiplies. Love allows you to tap into creative life force energy. And this is the very energy that was used to create universes!
Be grateful for the sanctity of love in its many forms. Honor it. Nurture it. Cultivate it. Help it grow.
