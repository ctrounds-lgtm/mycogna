At times, you use words that are semantically true but misrepresent your intention. An example would be a backhanded compliment; it seems positive but contains a hidden insult or criticism. Other examples would be flattery, manipulation or using technicalities to avoid taking responsibility.
To speak truth is to align your words with your intentions, even when the truth is difficult. This alignment engenders trust as your words come from a place of honesty and integrity.
Impeccability is the full alignment of thought, deed, and action. The old adage says, “If you don’t have something nice to say, don’t say anything at all.” Impeccability says, “If you don’t have something nice to think, don’t think anything at all.”
Living Fire
When was the last time you felt a fire in your belly? When was the last time a cause touched your heart so deeply it moved you to tears? When was the last time you felt your connection with Source / inner guidance so strongly that it inspired you to move boldly and take action?
Every person has a living fire inside of them. But for many, fear, doubt, and a sense of inadequacy causes the flame of certainty to dwindle. Tend to the embers in your heart. Fan the flame of your passion with a goal that is worthy of your greatness.
What is a worthy trade for your precious days on this planet? What goal is worth sacrificing your time, your energy, your well-earned money? Attend to the voice in your heart calling you to rise. The world needs your greatness. Nothing less than your best will suffice.
A great war is being waged. This battle is not being fought with guns. It is the war each person's soul fights as it struggles with complacency.
Your world is creating many distractions that are lulling you to sleep. It's time to wake up! Don't waste another moment of your precious life.
While conscious rest is just as important as conscious action, dis-traction is an insidious beast that slowly sucks your life-blood.
Tend to the garden that is seeded with your dreams and your passions. Water it every day. Fertilize the soil with your imagination and creativity. Breathe life-giving air into your heart-flame until it becomes an inferno. That, dear one, is the means by which the earth will be honed, refined, and purified.
The Fullness of Life’s Perfection
There is a message that you are being asked to deliver. There is a voice that only you can hear, one that offers words only you were meant to speak. Your message is unique, timely and necessary. There are those who need this message and they will find you.
Dropping into your heart, seeking the impeccable truth that lives there, you will find the strength and courage to speak words you may be hesitant to share. Do not be concerned when others speak more eloquently. There is grace in the simplicity of your truth. Your message doesn't need to be deeply profound or intellectual. It just needs to be yours.
People long for authenticity. They crave transparency. They need to know they are not alone in their suffering. It's your pain that makes you relatable. The fact that you can laugh through your pain is your true radiance.
There is no sky more beautiful than one lit with color after the storm. It's the clouds that make the sky interesting. Your face is like that sky. Your eyes reflect not only the brilliance of the sun, but the memory of the rain.
In your transparency lies the fullness of grace, a story of love, loss, healing and light. All of it is beautiful. There isn't a chapter in your life that wasn't perfect.
Kindness is the grace you offer yourself when you finally recognize the fullness of your life's perfection. Kindness is the grace you offer others as they are inspired to see the fullness of their own life's perfection.
Your Truest Gifts
The need to define your place and purpose is somewhat misguided. Your need to find purpose is sometimes rooted in doubt, or even lack of self-worth.
If you knew how very loved you are, how treasured you are, how cherished you are, how celebrated you are, questions of purpose would be rendered irrelevant. You are so infinitely worthy - right now, in this moment - of all the fabulous, incalculably valuable treasures you are receiving.
Every precious breath you draw, every magnificent perception of nature's glory, every radiant sight, sound, taste, smell and everything you feel on your skin, each exquisite pleasure you receive through your awe-inspiring body, is a gift that doesn't need to be earned. And these gifts are the ones that truly matter. To live is life's greatest gift, her greatest treasure.
Are you living? Or are you simply surviving? You will know the difference when you release the need to find your purpose. Living requires only your senses and the ability to tune into your body. Surviving is the struggle for achievement. Struggle in any form is a distraction that creates needless pain and longing. It also inspires hierarchy, evaluation, and judgement as you create a measuring stick to measure your own progress against that of others in your human family.
Turn away from your need for external validation. Turn away from your need to impress. Resist the impulse to tell your victim stories. Find beauty, grace, and satisfaction in that which is perceived through your senses and experienced through unwavering love.
Do not expect your love to be reciprocated. Simply allow an outpouring of love to wash through you, cleansing your heart, purifying your soul, and allowing you to perceive this world fully through your beautiful senses. Love is its own reward.
You are so very precious to all in the unseen world of spirit. Closer than your own skin, you are surrounded by a love so much more vibrant and alive than any human could possibly offer.
Turn your heart toward my love and receive full, complete and immediate healing. There is no need to suffer. Whatever your cross, lay it down. It is no longer yours to bear. Offer it up to my love and allow its energy to be recycled. All energy is love. By laying down your emotional burdens, you allow me to reclaim the energy of those negative emotions, restoring them to their natural state of love.
