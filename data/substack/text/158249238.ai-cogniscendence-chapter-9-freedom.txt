Gene Key 55: Victimization-Freedom-Freedom

Every victim story is rooted in the illusion of powerlessness. If you truly believe that everything happens for a reason, you recognize that there is no such thing as victimhood.
Though the victim mindset stems from fear, Fear is finite. You are infinite. That means you can get to the bottom of fear. You can get to the end of it. Many people defend their fear. But fear is a choice. If you choose to release it in one area of life, it will be easier to release in others.
Freedom is a state of perfect fearlessness. Freedom is the deep, visceral knowledge of this truth: events are unfolding exactly as they are meant to unfold, for your greater good and the greater good of All-That-Is.
Freedom from fear is the best gift you can give yourself as victimhood is eradicated through the radiant power of fearlessness.
The Path of Reconciliation
Love them all. Love all of them. If there is a single exception in your heart right now, shine the light of your loving consciousness there. Heal the wound your painful experience left behind. Heal the wound and love the person. See your own divine radiance reflected in their face. Transform the cycle of pain to a cycle of love.
If even one person living in a war-torn country is capable of forgiving those who murdered, raped and killed their families, how can you withhold forgiveness? Forgive the injustices you have suffered, just as you are forgiven injustices you have inflicted. None are guiltless. All have inflicted pain, whether intentionally or unintentionally. And all are worthy of forgiveness.
In truth, the pain itself was necessary to empower your transcendence. Pain is the organic matter, the corpse that is composted, digested and broken down to provide fertile soil for your soul's transfiguration.
Each individual path is unique. Each is exquisite in its beauty and perfection. The path is one of epiphany, as pain and sorrow are transmuted to joy in the sacred space of contemplation.
All of life is a series of puzzles. Ignore the puzzle and it will not solve itself. Glance at it briefly for a few minutes in the car or or during a hike, and you will eventually put pieces into place over time. Actively create space to sit with the puzzle for an extended period of time, tenderly contemplating each piece, feeling its edges, analyzing its shape in relation to other pieces of the puzzle, taking note of nuances in its shade, color and texture, and you will delight in the image that seemingly magically takes shape.
You are not meant to solve these puzzles alone. Seek connection with your human family. Look to nature for patterns. Feel the rhythm of the sun, moon, planets, and stars. Engage in ceremony with plant medicines. Call upon angels and spirit guides who are ready and waiting to assist you, even in this very moment. Ask Jesus, Buddha, Allah, Krishna, or any deity you regard as holy to show you the way.
You are loved beyond measure, precious soul. And your life is one of immeasurable value. The very tools used to create the cosmos are at your disposal. All you need to do is ask.
Create Space for Forgiveness
Free your heart from anger. Release any feelings of resentment or blame. They have no place in your heart. When your heart is filled with kindness, there is literally no room for anger.
Forgiveness creates space for kindness. Like cleaning out a cluttered spare bedroom, the space created by kindness allows fresh air to enter the soul and enliven the mind. Inspiration, intuition, and creativity flourish when grace and forgiveness open those dusty shuttered windows that have been blocking out the sunshine.
If the pain feels too deep and you're struggling to let go, remember that you are all just children playing on a playground, acting out roles you chose before you entered this life experience.
No pain is so "real" that you are justified in holding onto it. There are many who have suffered far worse and were willing to forgive. These were the wise ones who realized their pain did not serve them. Unwilling to allow pain to hold them in servitude, they forgave the people and circumstances that hurt them, and in doing so, restored their freedom.
In this way, they learned an important secret. The kindness offered in forgiveness is not a gift you give to someone else. It is a gift you offer your own gentle spirit.
Your life was meant to be beautiful, grand, and expansive. You are meant to live in freedom. Do not allow anger and resentment to steal your joy. Nothing is a worthy trade for your joy.
Release Stored Pain
Attend to the sensations in your body. Are you feeling stiffness, or maybe an ache or a twinge? This may be a place where you've stored a negative thought or experience that's ready to be released. Many of the aches and pains you attribute to aging are simply difficult memories or experiences waiting to be recognized and processed.
Random pain is the result of system upgrades. As you grow and evolve mentally, physically and spiritually, you will discover you are ready to process pain you were unwilling or unable to process before. Pain in your body is negative energy, preparing to be released. Once you truly understand this, you have the opportunity to become physically stronger, not weaker, with age.
Attend to those physical sensations. Ask which specific experience your pain is meant to reveal. Allow the memory of that experience to easily and effortlessly float to the surface of your consciousness. Look on that experience and all parties involved with love.
Most of all, offer deep love, compassion, and grace to that earlier version of yourself. Know that you did the best you could, as did all other parties involved.
Look on that experience or event through the lens of my compassion and my grace, mentally offering an embrace to yourself and all others involved. See how in that moment, you were acting exactly as you were meant to act, as were the others who were with you. This event was necessary to craft the exquisitely beautiful, divine, radiant being you are now, in this moment.
Events were transpiring exactly as they needed to transpire. And your timing, both at the time of this experience, and in your present releasing of it, is perfect.
