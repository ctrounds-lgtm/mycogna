The Performance of a Lifetime
Your world is addicted to drama. And there are so many forms of drama to choose from!
Everywhere you turn, there is opportunity to get sucked deeper into the illusion of separation; to believe the story that "other" is real, and "other" is responsible for your suffering. The more you refuse to play the victim, or refuse to identify with emotional upheaval, the more you will recognize the fact that ego, not other, is responsible for your emotional turbulence and drama.
There is a great shift occurring. It's been in the works for some time now. There are those who have felt it for years. As this shift gains momentum, more and more will feel it. For most, it will feel somewhat unpleasant, like the sensation you're on a merry-go-round that is going way too fast. It will feel as if life is spinning out of control.
But the discomfort isn't necessary. For those who choose to rise above the chaos, the ride itself is exhilarating. The secret is to see yourself as the composer of the musical score in your life's brilliant orchestration.
You chose the instruments. You selected the tempo. You wrote every note in one of the most fantastic compositions ever created. This is the song your soul was meant to sing. Each and every note is necessary in your exquisite masterpiece. You created the rise and fall of the melody. You created the dissonant tension. You created the dramatic pauses, followed by great bursts of energy. And you created the harmonic resolution.
When you step back and listen, you will see that all is in perfect balance. The musical score is divinely inspired. And you are offering the performance of a lifetime before millions of adoring fans.
Release Old Stories
Take a deep breath. Relax into this moment. Now think of your deepest desire. How pure is your energy as you think about the thing you most want?
Do you feel anxious, because achieving your dream requires talent, ability, or physical characteristics you aren't sure you possess? Do you feel tired, fearing you lack the energy or drive to accomplish the goals you are hoping to achieve? Do you make excuses as to why you don't deserve it, or feel that someone else deserves it more? Do you worry that the object of your desire will never come or that you are not destined to acquire it?
Release those thoughts now. Cast them off like a tattered, torn, over-used garment that no longer serves the purpose of protecting you and keeping you warm. In truth, it never served you. It was stealing your warmth, robbing you of your power. Relieved of the weight of that garment, continue on your path, free of its constrictions. Lighter now, walk into the warmth, the light, the power of your own magnificent sun.
Those old stories no longer hold power over you. Release them. Tell a new story, one in which you have all the talent, all the energy, all the worthiness and all the divine, radiant power to achieve your most cherished desire.
This desire lives in your heart because its fulfillment requires you to see yourself as you truly are – radiant, beautiful, divine, inspired, vibrant, and yes... worthy.
Path to Harmony
Your life is meant to be lived in joy. The secret to discovering joy is to play. The freedom to play is your birthright as a conscious creator on this magnificent planet. Look at the many avenues to joy – the many ways you can experience pleasure on this lovely earth.
There are so many things here to love: the animals, the plants, the birds, the trees, the flowers, the sunshine, the water, the air. But most of all, most of all, find joy in the company of your fellow creators – others who are expressions of the divine made manifest – others who share this planet with you.
Love them. Savor the exquisite beauty of their lives with them. Offer tenderness, support, and encouragement so they can feel safe, loved, and seen. This, dear soul, is the path to peace. This is the path to harmony on your beautiful planet.
When everyone shares in this joy and explores the freedom to play, all dis-harmony and dis-ease will simply disappear. Discord cannot exist in the presence of perfect harmony. And perfect harmony – the resonant frequency that you vibrate in your natural state of being – is so easily and effortlessly attained when you are attuned to this playful state of joy and well-being.
Reach for joy. Reach for pleasure. Reach for your highest expression of love. Peace and harmony on the planet will surely follow.
