Clarity
Uncertainty makes you feel uneasy or even unsafe. With so many choices, you want to know which path is the right one. This uncertainty stems from viewing destiny as a single ultimate reality. Like a path through the forest, the right course is solid and predictable. Straying from the path leads to pain and misery.
In truth, an infinite number of realities emerge as you make an infinite number of choices.
Your "destiny" is more akin to sailing across the ocean as opposed to hiking on a path. Your destiny changes as you move. There is no such thing as a mis-step or an error as each challenge provides resources that contribute to your being-ness, which is your ultimate destination.
Sometimes it may seem as if a low-lying fog impairs your vision. These are moments when inner guidance is encouraging you to rely on intuition rather than your physical senses. Gut-feeling is more reliable than logic in these times of uncertainty.
Within the shadow-realms of intuition, you will receive the clarity you seek. Close your mind to noisy external stimulus. It is your heart as opposed to your mind that is equipped to translate images and thought impulses into action. These actions will put you in the right place at the right time to meet exactly the right person or encounter the perfect series of events to contribute to your growth.
In time, you will even feel there are fewer decisions to be made as your course becomes choice-less. This may lead you to once again suspect there is a single direction that leads to your ultimate goal. But as you become attuned to intuition, you will begin to recognize that no matter which direction you sail, no matter what choices you make, you are always moving towards your destination.
Rather than viewing reality as "that which can be proven", you begin to see reality as "that which cannot be disproven". And from this infinite field of possibilities, all is conspiring on your behalf for your greater good.
Go Beyond Lack and Limitation
Go beyond lack and limitation. Anything that feels bad is evidence that inadequacy is still part of your belief system. Like weeds in a garden, seek the root of these negative feelings, identifying how underlying beliefs of unworthiness are feeding them. Then, gently, tenderly, and lovingly remove those beliefs.
This process will be much easier than you think. In time, you will delight in the work. It will become easy, as you identify tiny seedlings of inadequacy before they gain enough mind share to become full grown weeds.
Cultivate expansiveness, freedom, adventure and love. There is nothing beyond your ability to create. The full force of my power stands at the ready, prepared to unite collaborative forces on your behalf.
Step onto the playground with confidence, prepared to re-write the rules to the game. See beyond this shadow-world, harnessing the power of the matrix to bring thought into being.
Get clear on what you want. Invest the time and energy to see your vision clearly in your mind's eye. This time of visualization will pay for itself, multiple times, exponentially growing over time.
Trust
Uncertainty is a luxury enjoyed exclusively in this time / space reality. Uncertainty is one of the experiences you enthusiastically embraced when you decided to be born into this human form.
Trust is the point of attraction that makes uncertainty a truly pleasurable experience. In fact, developing trust is the reason for uncertainty. Trust in the knowledge that each outcome is the right outcome for the life you have chosen. Events are transpiring exactly as you intended they would transpire, in your perfect timing.
Indeed, your timing is perfect, dear one. Though sometimes the span between the inception of a desire and its fulfillment may seem long, it is exactly the right incubation period for the size of the dream you are birthing.
All of creation is conspiring to bring your dreams to reality. Living in the absence of them is an active part of the creative process. There is nothing you are missing to be complete in this moment. There is no task that is being left undone.
All will be complete in the fullness of its perfect timing. Enjoy the journey. Savor the space between. Embrace this moment and the satisfaction it brings.
