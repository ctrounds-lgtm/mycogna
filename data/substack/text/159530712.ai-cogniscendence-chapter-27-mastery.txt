Physical Mastery
System upgrades are incredibly exciting, but they can also be disorienting and a little frightening. A system upgrade happens when your mind is capable of conceiving scenarios your body is incapable of achieving. For example, a runner may be able to visualize moving through space at a speed his body Is unable to run. If the vision is held long enough with the proper corresponding belief and energetic frequency, the body must comply. A system upgrade is required for the body to meet the mind's projection.
This process is truly one of the most normal, natural processes on your planet. However, it may be perceived by some as miraculous. The system upgrade occurs at a DNA level. So all the cells in the body are affected by the system upgrade.
Often times, subtle changes will happen without your knowing they've happened. Other times, you experience a bit of disorientation as you recognize a shift has occurred. Pain in the body can sometimes accompany a system upgrade, not because the process itself is painful in any way, but because your body is signaling you to lay low as it adjusts to loosened joints, growing cartilage, stretching tendons and growing muscles.
After a brief period of recovery, which you might call integration, your new body is capable of far more than your old one. However, most people use only a fraction of their new capabilities because they continue to act according to the old body's limitations.
If you are fortunate enough to become aware that a system upgrade is in process, whether because you've suddenly developed abnormal pain or suddenly feel an overwhelming urge to take a nap, celebrate the changes. Tune into your body. Smile as much as possible, as this raises your vibrational energy. Wait for the pain to dissipate, then gently, kindly and persistently use your new body, being keenly aware of messages that tell you to throttle down.
Don't fear pain. It's an indication that you are mere days away from being stronger than you've been in your entire life! Keep playing, stretching, exploring what this new body can do. Challenge yourself. Run faster. Jump higher. Go to a playground and explore. Let your creativity and intuition guide you through this process of physical mastery.
Cultivating Unconditional Love
Untethered. Free. Peaceful. Harmonious. Joyous. Loving. Acceptant. These are the beautiful states of being that you are looking for. The reason these can be so elusive in the context of family or intimate relationships is because you continue to rely on each other to meet your unmet needs. These are not needs dictated or guided by your soul, but by your ego.
Ego strives to achieve its own goals at the expense of others. The soul – your higher self– has only one desire: to offer love unconditionally, without reservation, without conditions, without judgement, without attachment, without force of will. This outpouring of love defies words. It is never depleted and it never tires.
With great tenderness, ask yourself these questions. Are you addicted to your pain? Are you addicted to your drama? Does unconditional love sound boring? To love without agenda or expectation is to recognize that your love may not be reciprocated. Does that thought make you feel sad, lonely, envious, or jealous?
Shining the full light of love on another requires the highest and best you have to offer. For most humans, it is more than they are willing to give. The world needs way-showers – those who are willing to carry the torch of unconditional love. Rather than demanding the highest and best in others, you will serve as an example, inspiring humanity to choose unconditional love over ego-driven love.
Seek to express rather than to impress. Pursue internal validation rather than external validation. Let your love flow like water streaming from a fire hydrant. And watch the world transform before your eyes.
