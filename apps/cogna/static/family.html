What the Science Says

Story-telling isn't just meaningful.
It's medicine.

A clinical trial published in JAMA Network Open in April 2026 found that guided digital reminiscence reduced grief in dementia caregivers and strengthened the bond between patients and the people who love them. Researchers at Weill Cornell Medicine and USC demonstrated that revisiting memories together through a simple digital tool can ease grief and improve relationship quality.

"Many of the questions awakened responses and memories that I had never heard." — Participant, Living Memory Home clinical trial
Weill Cornell Medicine & USC, JAMA Network Open, April 2026

MyCogna was built on the same principle: the right question, asked at the right moment, can unlock a story that would otherwise disappear.
