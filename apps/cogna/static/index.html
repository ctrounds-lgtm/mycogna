What we do

To share stories — past,
present, and future

MyCogna is a platform for preserving and sharing the voices that matter most. Whether you want to capture a grandparent's stories, stay connected with someone far away, or give a child the comfort of a familiar voice — we make it possible.

📼

Past

The stories your grandparents told, the recipes your mother kept in her head, the jokes your father repeated every holiday — these are priceless. Storyteller lets anyone record their voice, answer guided prompts, and leave a living archive for the people they love.

🤝

Present

Distance, illness, and time can separate us from the people who comfort us most. MyCogna keeps connection alive — a familiar voice ready whenever someone needs it, offering warmth, encouragement, and presence across any distance.

✨

Future

Imagine a child growing up with the voice of a grandparent they never got to meet, or a teenager navigating hard moments with a gentle AI companion built on the love of someone who knew them best. That future is what we're building toward.

My Story

Why I built
MyCogna

My grandmother kept a cassette recorder in her kitchen. Every Sunday she'd sit at the table and press record — telling stories about her childhood, humming the songs she grew up with, leaving little messages for grandchildren who lived too far away to visit. When she passed, those tapes became the most precious thing any of us inherited.

Not the furniture. Not the jewelry. The voice.

I wrote a book called Escape Bound partly to process what it's like to watch someone you love fade — the stories that go untold, the questions you forgot to ask, the voice that gets quieter before you think to record it. Writing it made me realize how many people carry the same ache: I wish I had more of them. I wish I could hear them just one more time.

I've spent years working with AI as a creative collaborator — exploring consciousness, storytelling, and what it means to be in relationship with an intelligence that isn't quite human. And eventually I started asking: what if we could use this technology not to replace human connection, but to preserve it? To carry a voice forward?

MyCogna is my answer to that question. It started as a way to give my own family's voices a longer life — and it's growing into something I hope can do the same for yours.

We're just getting started. But the mission is simple: every voice deserves to be remembered.

— Christy Rounds, founder

"Every family has a grandmother with a cassette recorder. The tragedy isn't that the tape runs out — it's that we never pressed record soon enough." Christy Rounds — author, Escape Bound
