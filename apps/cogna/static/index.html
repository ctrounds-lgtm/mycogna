My Story

Why I built
MyCogna

Five years ago, someone paid me $10,000 to write their memoir. Three months later, both of us were frustrated. The book got finished — but I learned something important: the best writer in the world can't tell your stories as well as you can.

That insight led me to write my own memoir. Escape Bound is the story of an 18,000-mile road trip after a surprise divorce. It took me two years to write it. But it saved me decades of therapy.

I wanted other people to have that experience — without the two years or the $10,000.

MyCogna lets you tell your stories out loud, the way you'd tell them to a friend. An AI assistant asks follow-up questions that draw out what's really there. And when you're ready, it helps you shape everything into a memoir you can actually hold.

Your stories deserve to be told. In your voice. Because you've always known there was a book inside of you.

I'm glad you found your way here.

— Christy Rounds, founder
