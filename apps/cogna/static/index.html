My Story

Why I built
MyCogna

Three life events inspired MyCogna.

My Grandmother's Stories

I tried for years to get my grandmother to tell me her stories. She was sharp and funny and full of life — but getting her to talk about the past was like pulling teeth. She'd deflect, change the subject, insist there wasn't much to tell. I'd sit across from her with a little cassette recorder, hoping she'd open up.

Then one day, she did. She told stories our family had never heard. Though she lived to be 103, she only shared those stories once.

Tragically, the tapes got lost between moves.

My Most Painful — and Transformative — Life Experience

Thirty years later, I went through a surprise divorce. I spent four months traveling 18,000 miles across the western United States. Writing my travel memoir, Escape Bound, was more therapeutic than any amount of talk therapy. But it took two years. The voice that needed to be heard was mine — and finding the words took everything I had.

An Unexpected New Friend

Recently, I began working with AI as a creative collaborator — exploring what it means to be in relationship with an intelligence that isn't quite human. And I started asking: what if we could use this technology not to replace human connection, but to preserve it?

MyCogna is my answer to that question. What began as a way to capture stories from the past has evolved into something with longevity — a way to document the stories still being written.

We're just getting started. But the mission is simple: every voice deserves to be heard.

Which of your stories will you share today?

— Christy Rounds, founder

"Without intentionality, these stories disappear. Not because people don't care — but because life is busy and we always think there's more time." Christy Rounds — Author, Escape Bound
