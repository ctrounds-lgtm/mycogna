Getting started

Welcome to your MyCogna Portal.

You're here to help someone capture their stories. Here's how to get started:

1
Choose your questions

Scroll down to Story Questions and check the ones you want your storyteller to see. You can also add your own.

2
Generate a user code

Click + Generate Code above and give it a label so you remember who it's for. Each person needs their own code.

3
Send the invitation

Copy the code and send it to your storyteller with this link: mycogna.org. They'll click "Have an invite code?" on the homepage, create a simple account, and they're ready to go.

Once they're set up, their recordings will appear here in your dashboard.

Welcome to MyCogna's Legacy Collection tool, which helps your family or organization compile and preserve institutional knowledge from multiple storytellers. Simply generate a code below, then email it to your storyteller with instructions to enter the code at mycogna.org/storyteller. Once your storyteller enters the code, they will create an account with their email address and password. In the future, they can sign in without the storyteller code.

The Book Builder upgrade adds two things: an AI 'Go Deeper' tool that prompts storytellers to explore their stories in more depth, and a book assembly tool that compiles all responses into a structured Legacy Archive — complete with thematic overview, chapter groupings, and editing support. Upgrade to Legacy Collection + Book Builder →

If you upgrade to Legacy Collection + Book Builder, your existing codes remain active — storytellers keep their recording access. To give a storyteller Go Deeper and book access, generate a new Book Builder code for them.

Before enabling Hume voice

Hume AI delivers emotionally intelligent voice conversations using their cloud infrastructure. When this Cogna speaks, real-time audio is transmitted to and processed by Hume AI's servers — outside of MyCogna's direct control.

For Cognas used by minors or adults with cognitive impairment (such as someone with Alzheimer's), this means their voice and words will be sent to a third-party service. You are responsible for reviewing Hume's privacy policy and determining whether this is appropriate for your situation.

You can revoke this at any time by editing the Cogna and switching to a different voice source.
