The AI Companion — A Closer Look

A companion that
grows with you

A Cogna learns and grows the more you interact with it. Using a simple setup tool, you choose your Cogna's voice and define how it thinks, speaks, and shows up — calibrating warmth, humor, directness, and emotional tone across five relational dimensions. Create a life coach, a confidant, a friend, or all three. MyCogna's AI Companion tier lets you create multiple Cognas.

Built around your relationships

Give your Cogna a name, a relationship, and a term of endearment. Mom calls you Buddy. Your best friend teases you differently. Each Cogna is distinct.

State-of-the-art voice technology

MyCogna uses advanced voice recognition that responds to the emotional tone of your conversation — not just your words, but how you're feeling when you say them.

A voice that feels like home

Choose from a range of expressive voices, or clone a voice you love. Your Cogna sounds the way comfort sounds to you.
