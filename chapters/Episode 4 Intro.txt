I want to talk about time dilation and relativity. Relativistic time dilation shows that as an object approaches the speed of life, time slows down. According to The Twin Paradox, A twin traveling near the speed of light would age less than the twin staying on Earth, highlighting that high-speed travel acts as a journey into the future.

Inner space (the atomic/subatomic world) and outer space (the cosmos) share structural similarities. In many ways, they "mirror" each other. Both are composed of vast, empty spaces, with matter concentrated into tiny, dense structures.

Now I’m going to take a big intuitive leap. I suspect our minds provide a bridge to these inner and outer energy fields.

Three years ago, I was in an ayahuasca ceremony. For me, the entire experience felt like a journey through the inner cosmos where the shaman made stops that provided us the chance to heal the underlying archetypal wound that plagued our human relationships. Using his songs, or icaros, the shaman would call for rage, and all the people whose story included rage would purge. Then there was a sensation of acceleration and the spaceship would travel mind-bending distances to the next stop, which might be the stop for shame, for example. And the people experiencing deep guilt or shame would purge. 

I had the odd sensation that we were trading time for distance… that rather than using time to heal pain, the shaman was speeding up the healing process by exchanging distance for time. 

The concept seemed so crazy, I didn’t give it any thought. Until last summer when I read an article in the Scientific American. It suggested that at the event horizon, time and distance trade places. And if the inner world of our minds mirrors the outer world, maybe my idea isn’t as bizarre as it sounds.
